description: >-
  The United States Census Bureau, commonly referred to as the Census, is a
  government agency responsible for collecting and analyzing demographic data
  about the American population. Every ten years, the Census conducts a
  nationwide survey to gather information about the characteristics of the
  population, including age, gender, race, ethnicity, and household size. This
  data is used to determine the distribution of Congressional seats, allocate
  federal funding to states and communities, and inform decision-making in a
  wide range of policy areas. In addition to the decennial census, the Bureau
  also conducts various other surveys and reports throughout the year, providing
  valuable insights into the social, economic, and demographic trends shaping
  the United States. Overall, the Census plays a crucial role in shaping our
  understanding of the American population and guiding public policy at the
  national, state, and local levels.
