description: StanCorp Financial Group, Inc. (doing business as The Standard) is an insurance and financial services company headquartered in Portland, Oregon, and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Meiji Yasuda Life Insurance Company since 2016. Founded in 1906, The Standard offers group and individual disability insurance, group life and accidental death and dismemberment insurance, group dental and group vision insurance, absence management services, retirement plans, individual annuities, and commercial mortgage loan origination. The Standard does not offer a public developer API. Employer and benefits administrator integrations are managed through EDI and proprietary data exchange channels.
