      Gluu provides an open-source, cloud-native identity and access management
      platform that lets organizations secure web and mobile apps, APIs, and
      data with standards-based single sign-on, strong authentication, and
      fine-grained authorization. It implements protocols such as OpenID
      Connect, OAuth 2.0, SAML, FIDO2/WebAuthn, and UMA, integrates with
      directories like LDAP/Active Directory, and supports MFA, identity
      federation, and social login to deliver a unified sign-on experience.
      Deployed on Kubernetes or virtual machines, Gluu scales for both workforce
      and customer IAM use cases, offers policy-driven access control and
      consent, and includes tooling for administration and user self-service.
      The company also provides enterprise support and services around its
      distribution (e.g., Gluu Flex), which is built on the open-source Janssen
      Project.
