name: Product Backlog Items
description: Product backlog items (PBIs) are the individual units of work that make up a product backlog, representing features, enhancements, bug fixes, and technical tasks in Agile and Scrum methodologies. Each PBI typically includes a description, acceptance criteria, priority, and an estimate of effort. PBIs are continuously refined through grooming and refinement sessions, and the highest-priority items are pulled into sprints by development teams to be implemented and delivered as increments of working software.
