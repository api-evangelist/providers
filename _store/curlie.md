description: Curlie is a multilingual, human-edited web directory and the official successor to the Open Directory Project (ODP), also known as DMOZ. After the DMOZ directory was shut down in March 2017, the editor community relaunched the project as Curlie to continue cataloging websites by topic across more than 90 languages. Curlie is maintained entirely by volunteer editors who review and categorize submitted sites into a hierarchical taxonomy used as a reference by search engines and researchers.
