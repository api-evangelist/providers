name: Joint Fire Science Program
description: The Joint Fire Science Program (JFSP) funds scientific research on wildland fires and distributes results to help policymakers, fire managers and practitioners make sound decisions. Operated jointly by the U.S. Departments of the Interior and Agriculture, JFSP coordinates the Fire Science Exchange Network across regional knowledge nodes. JFSP does not publish a self-service developer portal or OpenAPI; research outputs are surfaced through the firescience.gov website.
