    How to read this without kidding yourself

    Rarity is the opportunityA check most of your market already passes buys you parity. A check nobody passes is a claim available to whoever ships first — and in this cohort those are the biggest awards left.
    Half credit is the cheapest groundWhere you already hold partial credit, something was found — a link, or an artifact written on your behalf. Moving it into the contract you serve is usually a header or a $ref, not a roadmap item.
    Verified beats documented, deliberatelyThe rubric grades a behaviour proven in the contract above the same behaviour asserted in prose. Publish the parameter, not the paragraph.
    This is a ceiling, not a promiseEach opening shows its full award. Graded checks pay that in full only when the evidence is in the contract, so treat every number here as the most it can be worth.
