{%- comment -%} THE COHORT PAYLOAD, emitted once and read by both market tools. Both sides of a market — priorities.html for buyers, opportunities.html for providers — render from THIS, which is the same site.data the ranked listing on the section page renders from. That is deliberate and it is the whole reason this include exists: the first version of the buyer tool pasted two CSVs out of a static bundle, so it froze on the day it shipped and drifted from the listing directly beneath it on every rescore. A tool that tells a buyer who to call, or a vendor what to fix, has to agree with the page it sits on. `total` and `shown` are carried through because the large industry listings are TRUNCATED at 1,000. On those pages every share, average and "who holds it" count describes the top 1,000 of a larger market, and both tools say so on the page rather than presenting a truncated cohort as the whole thing. Requires: page.data_key, page.cohort, page.section_url {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign listing = site.data[page.data_key] -%}
