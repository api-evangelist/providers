description: Limbic is a clinical AI company for mental healthcare, founded in 2020 and headquartered in New York with a London engineering base. Its two products — Limbic Access, a Class IIa (UKCA) medical device for conversational patient intake, assessment and clinical decision support, and Limbic Care, an AI companion delivering guided CBT and on-demand support between therapy sessions — are embedded by health plans, health systems, behavioral health providers and NHS Talking Therapies services. Both are built on the proprietary "Limbic Layer", a clinical reasoning layer that sits between the user and a large language model to enforce validated clinical guidance. Limbic Access embeds into a provider website with a small script snippet rather than a public developer API; the company publishes no developer portal, OpenAPI definitions or SDKs, and integrates with EHR and patient management systems through customer engagements. Research code accompanying its peer-reviewed studies (including
