description: Limbic is a clinical AI company for mental healthcare, founded in 2020 and headquartered in New York with a London engineering base. Its two products — Limbic Access, a Class IIa (UKCA) medical device for conversational patient intake, assessment and clinical decision support, and Limbic Care, an AI companion delivering guided CBT and on-demand support between therapy sessions — are embedded by health plans, health systems, behavioral health providers and NHS Talking Therapies services.
