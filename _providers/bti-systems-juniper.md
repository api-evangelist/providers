description: BTI Systems was an Ottawa, Ontario based supplier of cloud and metro packet-optical networking systems and software, best known for the BTI 7800 Series Packet Optical Transport platform, the BTI 800 Series, and the proNX family of management software (proNX Service Manager and proNX Optical Director). Juniper Networks acquired the remaining ownership interest in BTI Systems for approximately $25.8 million in cash, closing the acquisition on April 1, 2016, to accelerate open and automated packet-optical transport and SDN/NFV capabilities. BTI no longer operates as an independent company and publishes no independent developer portal or public API; the BTI Series and proNX product lines are documented and supported under Juniper Networks, whose proNX Optical Director exposes REST APIs northbound for OSS/BSS integration with NETCONF and telemetry southbound.
