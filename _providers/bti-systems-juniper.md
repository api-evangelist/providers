BTI no longer operates as an independent company and publishes no independent developer portal or public API; the BTI Series and proNX product lines are documented and supported under Juniper Networks, whose proNX Optical Director exposes REST APIs northbound for OSS/BSS integration with NETCONF and telemetry southbound.
