description: 6 Wunderkinder (6Wunderkinder GmbH) was a Berlin-based software startup founded in 2010 that built Wunderlist, a popular cross-platform to-do list and task management application. The company shipped a public REST API for Wunderlist in 2013, along with webhooks and integrations such as Slack and Sunrise, and rebuilt its backend into roughly 60 microservices behind a single unified API.
