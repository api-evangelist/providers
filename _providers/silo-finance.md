description: Silo Finance is a decentralized, non-custodial lending protocol built around isolated money markets. Each market pairs a single collateral asset with a borrowable asset in its own risk silo, so the failure or manipulation of one asset cannot cascade into unrelated markets — the isolation model that distinguishes Silo from pooled lenders. The protocol is delivered as onchain smart contracts (Silo V1, V2 and V3) deployed across Ethereum and other EVM chains, with an application at silo.finance, developer and protocol-architecture documentation at docs.silo.finance, third-party security audits, and an open GitHub organization publishing the contracts, subgraph and indexer.
