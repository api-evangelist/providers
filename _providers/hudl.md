description: Hudl is a Lincoln, Nebraska sports performance and analytics technology company founded in 2007. The company started as a video sharing and review platform for high school, college, and professional teams and has grown into a global provider of video capture hardware, performance analysis software, and sports data services. Hudl operates across basketball, baseball, football, ice hockey, rugby, soccer, softball, and volleyball, serving customers from youth clubs through professional leagues. The company's product portfolio spans the core Hudl video platform, Hudl Focus AI-powered capture cameras (Indoor, Outdoor, Flex, Point), Hudl Replay and Hudl Studio for live and tactical analysis, Hudl IQ for American football analytics, Volleymetrics, the Hudl TV livestreaming product, and Hudl Tickets digital ticketing. Through acquisitions Hudl has consolidated several leading performance analysis and football data brands under its umbrella, including Sportscode (performance analysis
  software), Wyscout (the largest soccer video and stats database), Statsbomb (advanced football analytics), Instat (video and scouting), WIMU and Titan (athlete tracking and wearables), and Signal and ADI. Hudl exposes commercial developer surfaces primarily through Hudl Wyscout (Wyscout Data API, documented as OpenAPI 3.0 at apidocs.wyscout.com, with Database, Stats, Events, and Physical Data packs) and Hudl Statsbomb (the Statsbomb Aggregated API for player-match, player-season, team-match, and team-season data, accessed via the Data Hub, plus the Raw X&Y Tracking Data API and a Wyscout/Statsbomb ID Mapping API). Core Hudl video and Hudl Focus do not publish a public developer API; B2B integrations are sales-mediated. Hudl maintains a public GitHub organization (github.com/hudl) with infrastructure, resilience, and media tooling open source projects (Mjolnir, Fargo, HudlFfmpeg, react-native components), but these are engineering libraries, not product SDKs. Hudl's revenue model is SaaS
  subscriptions for video, analytics, and data packages, plus camera hardware sales and data licensing.
