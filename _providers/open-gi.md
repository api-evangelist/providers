description: Open GI is a Worcester-headquartered insurance software house that has supplied the UK and Ireland general insurance market for more than 45 years, serving over 600 broker, MGA and insurer customers across personal and commercial lines. Its API posture is honestly partner-gated. Mobius genuinely ships APIs — Open GI's own public release notes describe Client Checks / sanctions-check endpoints and an API-triggered custom-event call — but the Mobius Developer Portal is a customer-only surface whose URL Open GI does not publish, no developer, docs, api or partners subdomain resolves on
