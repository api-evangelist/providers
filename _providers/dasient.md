description: Dasient was a web anti-malware company founded in 2008 in Sunnyvale, California by ex-Google engineers Neil Daswani and Shariq Rizvi with McKinsey consultant Ameet Ranadive. It sold cloud-based protection against web-based malware and malvertising, scanning customer websites and ad networks with its Malware Analysis Platform. It shipped a JSON-RPC over HTTP "Web Protection API" that let customers schedule and run on-demand malware scans of individual domains and URLs, with tiered pricing by scans per day. Twitter acquired Dasient in January 2012 for a reported $19.1 million; the product was retired, dasient.com went dark in late 2012, and the domain is now a parked defensive registration held by Twitter.
