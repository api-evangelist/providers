Its patent-pending Dropoff SDK runs on a delivery driver''s smartphone and uses sensor fusion to detect delivery events indoors — parking location, building entrance, elevator or staircase, floor level, and the final doorstep — even in GPS-degraded environments where satellite positioning fails.
