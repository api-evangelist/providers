description: Wallet Studio, built by Miss Moneypenny Technologies GmbH, is a no-code SaaS platform for creating, deploying, and managing Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes. Aimed primarily at insurance carriers, it turns policy administration, claims, and CRM systems into wallet-based customer engagement channels reaching the four billion people who already carry a digital wallet, without replacing existing backend infrastructure. The platform offers visual pass editors with one-click publishing, prebuilt insurance templates, integrated automation, a REST API and webhooks for connecting to third-party systems, and authenticated links to portals and value-added services. Hosting is GDPR-compliant and the company describes the platform as certified for enterprise security standards. Backed by Earlybird, the company raised roughly EUR 7 million in 2025.
