Its product reduced log and event volume (filtering low-value data by up to ~80%), normalized diverse telemetry formats into standardized schemas such as OCSF, and enriched data at ingest before it reached a SIEM or observability backend. Founded to tame the roughly 35% annual growth of security and observability data, the company was backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners and was subsequently acquired by SentinelOne, where the technology now ships as Singularity AI Data Pipelines.
