description: Kiwilogic.com AG was a German conversational-AI pioneer founded in 1999 in Hamburg, with a secondary office in London. It built the Lingubot platform, one of the earliest commercial natural-language dialogue engines for the web, which let online businesses deploy interactive virtual characters that acted as store greeter, customer-support agent, product specialist and entertainer across web sites and mobile messaging channels. The company was backed by Earlybird Venture Capital (approximately $5.2M reported) and won a best-German-startup award in 2000. The Lingubot business was acquired in the mid-2000s and folded into Artificial Solutions, whose Teneo conversational-AI and voice-AI platform is the commercial successor; the kiwilogic.com domain now issues a blanket redirect to teneo.ai. Kiwilogic operates no independent developer portal, documentation, or API surface today, and this profile is maintained as a historical company record within the API Evangelist network rather
