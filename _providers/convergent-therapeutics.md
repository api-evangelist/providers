description: Convergent Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company based in Cambridge, Massachusetts, developing next-generation targeted radiotherapies for cancer. Founded in 2020 by Philip Kantoff, the company builds alpha radioantibodies that pair a humanized monoclonal antibody against prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA) with the alpha emitter actinium-225, delivering short-range, high-energy alpha radiation to tumor cells. Its lead candidate, CONV01-alpha (rosopatamab tetraxetan), is in the Phase 2 CONVERGE-01 trial in metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer, including patients previously treated with Lu-177 PSMA radioligand therapy, with a pivotal Phase 3 planned. Convergent announced a $90 million Series A financing in 2023.
