Founded in 2020 by Philip Kantoff, the company builds alpha radioantibodies that pair a humanized monoclonal antibody against prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA) with the alpha emitter actinium-225, delivering short-range, high-energy alpha radiation to tumor cells.
