description: DeepScribe is a San Francisco, California ambient AI medical scribe company that automates clinical documentation and coding for specialty healthcare providers. Founded in 2017 (incorporated 2019) by Matthew Ko, Kairui Zeng, and Akilesh Bapu, DeepScribe captures the natural conversation between clinician and patient and produces structured clinical notes inside the provider's existing EHR workflow. The platform is HIPAA-compliant and is deployed across more than 1,500 healthcare organizations, with reported traction of 8 million+ visits captured and a 99.92% note-satisfaction rating. Core products include the AI Medical Scribe (ambient documentation), SmartPrep (pre-visit intelligence), AI Coding (E/M, ICD-10, and HCC automation), Customization Studio (provider-specific note personalization), and DeepScribe Assist (point-of-care insights). DeepScribe ships bi-directional EHR integrations with Epic, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, NextGen, AdvancedMD, drchrono, ModMed, Flatiron
  (OncoEMR), Ontada (iKnowMed Generation 2), and Objective Medical Systems, with a focused practice in oncology, cardiology, gastroenterology, neurology, orthopedics, urology, and value-based care. The company partners with AWS, Anthropic, Deloitte, and Panda Health for infrastructure, model, and go-to-market support. DeepScribe is sold as enterprise SaaS through a contact-sales motion; there is no public developer API, SDK, or open-source release, and authentication into the clinician workspace happens through canvas.deepscribe.ai.
