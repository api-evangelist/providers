description: Circular was a Spanish hiring community that connected tech recruiters with software developers, describing itself as a "Hiring Community for Tech Recruiters & Developers." It operated a referral-driven talent network in which recruiters shared vetted candidates they could not place themselves, and developers received job opportunities through that trusted pool rather than through cold outreach. The company was backed by Point Nine Capital and ran its candidate and recruiter landing experiences on a Next.js application served from circular.io and static.circular.io. Circular has since shut down and merged into Manfred (getmanfred.com), the largest tech community in Spain, with its own migration notice stating "Circular cierra sus puertas para integrarse en Manfred." The circular.io domain remains registered to Singular People Europe S.L. but has been fully undelegated, and Point Nine no longer lists Circular in its portfolio.
