description: AirBand Communications, Inc. was a fixed-wireless broadband provider for business customers, founded in 2000 by Andy Lombard and headquartered in Dallas, Texas. It delivered high-speed data, Voice over IP, security and managed services over a last-mile fixed-wireless network, bypassing incumbent local phone companies, and operated in metropolitan markets including Baltimore, Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, Philadelphia and Phoenix. The company was venture-backed, raising roughly $119M across sixteen rounds from investors including Battery Ventures, M/C Partners and Crescendo Ventures, and grew by acquiring the fixed-wireless business of WindChannel Communications (2006) and Sparkplug (2010). Airband was acquired by United Network Services (UNSi) on 2013-05-15 and no longer operates independently.
