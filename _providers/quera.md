description: QuEra Computing is a Boston-area neutral-atom quantum computing company, founded in 2021 by scientists from Harvard and MIT. Its flagship system, Aquila, is a 256-qubit field-programmable analog neutral-atom quantum processor that has been publicly available since November 2022 through Amazon Braket and through a direct Premium Access channel. QuEra develops Bloqade, an open-source software stack for neutral-atom quantum computing delivered as a Python SDK (Bloqade Python) and as the original Bloqade.jl Julia package, along with surrounding tooling for circuits, analog control, atom shuttling, decoders, and tensor-network simulation. The company is building toward fault-tolerant quantum computing with its Gemini-class next-generation systems and has demonstrated logical-level magic state distillation on neutral-atom hardware. QuEra works with HPC centers, national labs (NERSC, Los Alamos), DARPA, NEDO/AIST, NVIDIA, AWS, BCG X, Deloitte, Classiq, Kipu Quantum, Wolfram Research,
