description: Privado (Privado.ai) is a privacy engineering and data-privacy compliance company that helps engineering and privacy teams build privacy-first products. Its flagship is an open-source static code-analysis scanner that detects personal-data flows in source code, finds data-security violations, and generates compliance artifacts such as RoPA and Google Play Store Data Safety reports. The scanner runs locally or in CI/CD (Docker-based) via the Privado CLI, and results can optionally sync to the Privado Cloud dashboard where products like Wren (an AI privacy analyst), Agentic Assessments, Dynamic Data Maps, Web Auditor, and App Auditor automate data mapping and privacy impact assessments. Privado is a DevSecOps / privacy-governance tool rather than a public REST API provider; its developer surface is the open-source scanner, the CLI, and the docs at docs.privado.ai.
