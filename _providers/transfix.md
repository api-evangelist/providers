The Transfix TMS unifies pricing, planning, execution, and billing in a single platform, with feature areas covering Smart Pricing and Quoting, RFP Management and Collaboration, Coverage and Carrier Management, Streamlined Scheduling, Operations and Automation, Invoicing and Payments, and Reporting and Insights. Core AI capabilities include the Custom Rate Prediction Suite for spot and contract pricing across Dry Van, Reefer, and Flatbed equipment, and the RFP Workflow with a Smart Upload feature that structures bid data from heterogeneous file formats. Transfix markets integrations with existing TMS, carrier networks, and data sources,
