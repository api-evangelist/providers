Founded around 2011 and backed by Uncork Capital (then SoftTech VC), card.io was acquired by PayPal in 2012. Its scanning SDKs for iOS, Android, Cordova, and React Native were open-sourced in December 2014 under the card-io GitHub organization, and the product was ultimately discontinued. The company operated no public REST or web API; its product surface was a set of client-side mobile SDKs, all of which are now archived.
