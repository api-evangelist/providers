description: Fashionara was an Indian premium online fashion retailer that launched in 2012 as a curated "e-fashion mall" selling ladies, men's and kids apparel, footwear and lifestyle goods. It ran a flash-sale "events" model on a Magento storefront, carried more than 200 international labels alongside Indian brands including Kenneth Cole, Calvin Klein, Fossil, Adidas, Benetton, Van Heusen, Allen Solly, Clarks and Arrow, and operated a quirky in-house sub-brand called F.lea Bazaar. It offered free shipping over Rs. 799, free returns, next-day delivery in eight Indian cities, a style-advisor booking service and an Android app (com.fnara.main). The company was backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners. Fashionara never published a public API, developer portal, SDK or OpenAPI definition — a scan of more than 8,000 archived fashionara.com URLs found no /api, /developer, /docs, /swagger, /oauth or /graphql paths, and no api/developer subdomain was ever archived. The storefront went dark in mid-2016
