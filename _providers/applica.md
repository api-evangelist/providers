description: Applica was an AI-based document understanding and text automation company founded in Warsaw, Poland, built on deep-learning research in natural language processing for unstructured documents. Its platform read, interpreted, and extracted information from documents such as invoices, contracts, insurance claims, legal documents, and financial filings across the finance, insurance, legal, and media industries. Applica went through the Barclays Accelerator powered by Techstars and raised a Series A led by Cogito Capital Partners in 2020. Snowflake announced its intent to acquire Applica in August 2022 and absorbed the technology into the Snowflake platform. Applica no longer operates as an independent product or API provider, and applica.ai is no longer a live developer surface; this profile is retained for historical/network provenance.
