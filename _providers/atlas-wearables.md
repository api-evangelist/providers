description: Atlas Wearables was an Austin, Texas fitness-technology startup founded in 2013 by Peter Li, Mike Kasparian, and Alex Hsieh. It raised over $500,000 on Indiegogo in 2014 for the Atlas Wristband, a motion-tracking gym wearable whose "Exercise ID" technology automatically recognized strength exercises and counted reps and sets. The company shipped the Atlas Wristband and Wristband 2 and historically promoted an open developer API and integrations with apps such as MapMyFitness and Fitocracy.
