The Halan app and its supporting agent network reportedly serve 5–7 million customers, have disbursed in excess of USD 4 billion in loans across 3 million borrowers, and process 100+ million USD in monthly
  payments and 50+ million USD in monthly e-commerce sales. After raising USD 400 million in 2023 to cross unicorn status and an additional USD 157.5 million in 2024 (including an IFC investment), MNT-Halan expanded beyond Egypt into Turkey (via the August 2024 acquisition of Tam Finans), the UAE (December 2024 launch), and Pakistan. MNT-Halan's revenue model is interest and fees on lending, merchant and payment fees, e-commerce gross margin, and card interchange; the company markets directly to consumers and SMEs through its Halan app and physical outlets, and does not publish a public developer API, SDK, partner sandbox, or open-source code repository.
