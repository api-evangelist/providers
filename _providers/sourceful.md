description: Sourceful is a Manchester, United Kingdom packaging technology company that began as a sustainable packaging marketplace connecting brands with vetted manufacturers and offering data-driven carbon footprint, life-cycle, and recyclability metrics for every packaging design. Founded in 2020 by Wing Chan and Charlie Bradshaw, the company raised roughly $32M from investors including Index Ventures, Coatue, and Eka Ventures, and grew to over 100 employees serving climate-conscious consumer brands. Since 2024–2025 Sourceful has pivoted toward AI-powered visual design for packaging, releasing Sourceful 3.0 and Riverflow 1, a proprietary image-editing foundation model marketed as state-of-the-art for design-grade, print-ready packaging artwork. The current product line covers AI logo design, packaging design, AI product photography, image editing, packaging ranges, flavor/variant ranges, brand moodboards, and (announced) product mockups and social ads. Sourceful is ISO 42001 certified
  for AI management.
