The company builds smart robotic cells for surface treatment and finishing in high-mix, high-variability manufacturing — sanding, grinding, polishing, buffing, blasting, spraying, trimming and AI-based dimensional inspection and defect detection — pairing off-the-shelf industrial robot arms, 3D scanners and tools with its own physics-informed AI stack (GMR-AI, marketed as Factory SuperIntelligence / FSI). GrayMatter sells robots-as-a-service on a monthly subscription that
  bundles hardware, software, training, remote monitoring and 24/7 support instead of an upfront capital purchase, and counts Boeing, Caterpillar and Oshkosh among its customers. GrayMatter Robotics is a robotics hardware and AI systems manufacturer: as of the August 2026 enrichment pass it publishes no public developer API, developer portal, SDK, CLI or machine-readable API contract (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, gRPC), no MCP server and no A2A agent card. Note that this company is unrelated to greymatter.io, the service-mesh vendor with a similar name.'
