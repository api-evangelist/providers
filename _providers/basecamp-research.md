description: Basecamp Research is a London-based biotech company mapping global genetic biodiversity to power AI-based design of biological systems. Its core asset is BaseGraph, a Neo4j-backed knowledge graph of billions of biological relationships assembled from novel protein and genome sequences collected under access-and-benefit-sharing partnerships with biodiversity-rich countries.
