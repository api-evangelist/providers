description: Iantrek, Inc. is a venture-backed ophthalmic medical technology company headquartered in White Plains, New York, pioneering the field it calls bio-interventional ophthalmic surgery (BIOS) for the treatment of glaucoma. Founded in 2019 by ophthalmic innovator Sean Ianchulev, MD, MPH, the company develops micro-interventional surgical devices that reinforce and restore the eye's natural aqueous outflow pathways with biologic allograft tissue rather than bypassing or removing tissue. Its commercial and pipeline portfolio includes AlloFlo Uveo, AlloSert Uveo, C-Rex and AlloSpan Canal. Iantrek has raised approximately $103M across its rounds, including a $42M Series C used to launch AlloFlo Uveo in the United States in October 2025.
