Its product lines span Radio Access Network (4G/5G RAN), fiber broadband access (FTTx/GPON), packet-optical and DWDM/OTN transport (TJ1600), converged broadband (TJ1400), switching and routing, mobile packet core, satcom, and Direct-to-Mobile broadcast, all managed through the TejNMS (TJ5500) network management system.
