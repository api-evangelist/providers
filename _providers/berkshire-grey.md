description: Berkshire Grey is a Bedford, Massachusetts-based AI and robotics company that builds integrated pick-and-place, parcel sortation, trailer-unloading, and shuttle-sortation systems for eCommerce fulfillment, retail replenishment, and parcel logistics operations. Its product portfolio is organized around four flagship robotic systems — Core robotic picking system, Dispatch parcel sorter, Scoop trailer unloader, and Stride shuttle sorter — supported by the BG Flex mobile robotic platform and a centralized operating environment that orchestrates fleets of industrial-grade mobile robots for induct, transport, sort, sequence, and fulfill workflows including buy-online-pickup-in-store, curbside, and store replenishment. The company holds 700+ awarded patents (300+ pending) and has deployed solutions with Global-100 retailers and logistics service providers including Walmart, Target, and FedEx, with FedEx expanding from small-parcel sortation into broader AI robotic package-handling
  capabilities. Berkshire Grey originally went public via SPAC merger with Revolution Acceleration Acquisition Corp in 2021, then entered into a definitive merger agreement with SoftBank Group in 2023 (approximately $375M / $1.40 per share) and is now operated as a private SoftBank portfolio company.
