description: Jingle is a consumer marketplace mobile app for fast, direct local deliveries from mobile stores and services — "shops-on-wheels" — operating in the San Francisco Bay Area. Users sign up with a phone number (no password) and enable push notifications to be alerted when their favorite mobile vendors are nearby — artisanal food trucks, farmers'-market sellers, and one-click services such as bike repair, car wash, manicures, pedicures, and knife sharpening — then request a "jingle" to summon them. Deliveries are handled directly by the businesses themselves, cutting out delivery middlemen. Merchants use the app at their discretion to broadcast their presence to nearby neighborhoods, and residential complexes can partner to bring vendors on-site. Jingle is a portfolio company of Bessemer Venture Partners. The company publishes no public API, developer portal, or SDK; this profile captures its public consumer and merchant surface.
