description: SINAI Technologies is a San Francisco–headquartered enterprise SaaS company providing a Decarbonization Intelligence platform for carbon management, sustainability planning, and ESG compliance. The platform unifies organization-wide emissions accounting, climate transition planning, climate financial planning, and execution management so industrial enterprises can measure, report, and reduce Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions using science-based methodologies. Core modules include Carbon Accounting & Reporting, the Climate Transition Planner (with marginal abatement cost curves and scenario modeling), the Climate Financial Planner, and Execution & Management for tracking decarbonization projects. SINAI's Decarbonization Intelligence Portal layers Tableau-based dashboards, AI-powered bulk data ingestion, and AI Emissions Match (which maps unstructured emissions data to emission factors) on top of the underlying carbon ledger. The platform is designed around major reporting frameworks
  including CDP, TCFD, SEC climate disclosure, CSRD, CBAM, California SB 253 / SB 261, Brazil SBCE, and Brazil CVM resolutions 217 and 218, and is SOC 2 compliant with TÜV SÜD ISO 14064 verification. Customers include Siemens Energy, ArcelorMittal, Harley-Davidson, Emirates, Natura &Co, Minerva Foods, Wilson Sons, BRK Ambiental, and Japan's largest bank MUFG. SINAI states publicly that it can connect to enterprise customer systems through APIs to automate roughly 70% of emissions data ingestion, but it does not publish a developer portal, OpenAPI specification, SDKs, public API reference, or a GitHub organization — its API surface is delivered as a private integration capability negotiated with enterprise customers rather than a self-serve developer product.
