description: ClearMotion is an automotive motion-technology company founded out of MIT in 2012 by Zack Anderson and Shakeel Avadhany, headquartered at its US innovation lab in Billerica, Massachusetts, with a UK engineering hub at MIRA Technology Park in Nuneaton, a Shanghai office, and a production plant in Changshu, China. Its CM1 fully active suspension system pairs the ActiValve digital hydraulic actuator with software that counteracts road disturbances in milliseconds, and its RoadMotion service is a crowdsourced, cloud-based road-surface intelligence layer that fuses data from vehicle sensors into a high-definition surface map accurate to better than 20mm, feeding proactive suspension, chassis and ADAS decisions. The company absorbed the Bose Ride and Project Sound active-suspension programs, is led by CEO Christian Steinmann, and ships with NIO (ET9, ES9) alongside collaborations with Porsche, BWI Group and Sona Comstar. Marketing material describes RoadMotion as an "end-to-end cloud
  API solution", but ClearMotion publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, machine-readable specification, SDKs or /.well-known discovery surface — the API is an OEM/B2B integration rather than a self-serve public API.
