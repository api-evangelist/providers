The platform records assets, transfers, transactions, and supplier form data against verifiable identities for smallholder farmers, waste pickers, and other last-mile suppliers, and layers on compliance and reporting products for EUDR due diligence, Extended Producer Responsibility, B-BBEE, Scope 3 carbon accounting, lifecycle assessment, plastic and carbon credits, living income and wage, and anti-forced/child-labour screening.
