description: Raken is a San Diego-based construction technology company providing a field-first daily reporting and field management platform for general contractors, subcontractors, and heavy-civil contractors. The Raken mobile and web apps let field crews capture daily progress reports, time cards, production quantities, safety observations, toolbox talks, photos, videos, and checklists, then push that data back to the office in real time. Office users get dashboards across projects, members, equipment, materials, budgets, and certifications, and can sync data with accounting, payroll, and project management tools such as QuickBooks, Sage, Foundation, Procore, Autodesk, and Bluebeam. Raken also exposes a Public API 3.0 — a REST API protected by OAuth 2.0 — covering projects, members, daily reports, shifts, time cards, work logs, equipment logs, material logs, observations, checklists, toolbox talks, certifications, groups, classifications, cost codes, pay types, budgets, and more, enabling
  third-party integrations and custom workflow automation across the construction back office.
features:
- Daily progress reporting with notes, photos, and videos that sync from mobile to web in real time
- Auto-generated, brandable PDF daily report exports for owners, GCs, and clients
- Time cards and time tracking with classifications, cost codes, pay types, and crew-based or per-worker entry
- Production tracking against cost codes and budgets with units-installed and quantities-completed
- Equipment tracking and equipment logs with idle and active hours
- Material tracking, material units, and material logs
- Safety management with observations, incident logging, and safety dashboards
- Toolbox talks (current and past) library and sign-off workflow
- Quality management with digital checklists and checklist templates
- Resource scheduling and workforce planning
- Project management module with RFIs and document management
- Production insights and project-level analytics dashboards
- Member management with certifications, groups, and project membership in batch
- Public API 3.0 over REST with OAuth 2.0 (authorization code) and Bearer tokens
- Webhook-free polling model with per-second, concurrent-burst, and daily quota rate limits
- Native iOS and Android mobile apps, plus full-featured web app
- Integrations across accounting/payroll (Sage, QuickBooks, Foundation, Deltek ComputerEase, Viewpoint, Paychex, Points North, CMiC), project management (Procore, Autodesk, Bluebeam), cloud storage (Egnyte, Google Drive, Box, Dropbox, OneDrive), and reality capture (EarthCam, DroneDeploy, HoloBuilder, TrueLook)
- Open-source MobX state libraries (mobx-mc) published under the rakenapp GitHub organization
