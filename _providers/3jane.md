description: 3Jane is an Ethereum-mainnet credit-based money market that issues algorithmic, real-time uncollateralized USDC credit lines underwritten against verifiable financial proofs rather than on-chain collateral. Its 3CA credit-assessment algorithm and Jane Score combine on-chain history with off-chain signals (bank data via Plaid, VantageScore/credit metadata, zkTLS proofs via Reclaim Protocol and EigenLayer). Capital suppliers deposit USDC to mint USD3, a yieldcoin with a senior claim on interest repayments, or stake it for sUSD3, a junior tranche that earns levered yield while absorbing first losses. Backing comes from Fintech Credit Conduits (warehouse loans and forward-flow programs for U.S. fintech lenders) and Crypto Credit Lines. The protocol is governed by a timelock and multisig and coordinated by the JANE token. 3Jane raised a $5.2M seed round led by Paradigm, with Coinbase Ventures and Wintermute Ventures participating.
