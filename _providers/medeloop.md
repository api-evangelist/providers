Its products include Medeloop Analytics (AI agents that analyze EHR, claims, and registry data without requiring users to code), EvidenceKit (analytics paired with all-payer claims data), Medeloop Grants (AI-assisted grant discovery and proposal writing), and Medeloop Care (population health and care management). It serves pharmaceutical and life-sciences companies, health systems, and clinical
