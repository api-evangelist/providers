description: Spiral (SpiralDB) is a multimodal data platform built for the "machine consumer" — frontier AI systems and agents. It unifies video, images, audio, embeddings, point clouds, sensor streams, tables, and text under one query language and coordinate-frame system, serving everything from raw bytes to model-ready tensors at machine-scale throughput for pretraining pipelines and agentic access. Spiral is the creator of Vortex, a high-performance extensible columnar data format (100x faster random access and 10-20x faster scans vs. Apache Parquet) now incubating under the Linux Foundation, along with the FastLanes, FSST, and ALP compression libraries. The hosted Spiral warehouse is currently invite-only (Request Access); the public developer surface today is the open-source Vortex ecosystem (Python and Rust bindings).
