Founded in 2004 by Steve Hafner and Paul English, KAYAK aggregates results from hundreds of online travel agencies, airlines, hotel chains, car-rental companies, and cruise providers to help travelers compare prices for flights, hotels, cars, vacation packages, and cruises across a single interface. The company operates the KAYAK consumer brand, the HotelsCombined and Momondo meta-search brands, and KAYAK for Business, a corporate travel platform. KAYAK also runs a global Affiliate Network that lets publishers, content sites, and travel partners embed KAYAK search via co-branded search forms, iframes, deep links, and white-label search pages, with revenue share on redirected click traffic. KAYAK does not publish a self-serve public REST or GraphQL API for third-party developers; the consumer experience is delivered through its websites, native apps,
  voice integrations (Alexa, Google Assistant), and chat surfaces (Slack, Messenger). Programmatic access is limited to vetted partners under the KAYAK Affiliate Network and KAYAK Partner Network agreements, which typically expose XML/JSON feeds, deep-link builders, white-label search results pages, and conversion tracking — all gated behind a partner application process. There is no public OpenAPI specification, no published SDK, and no developer portal for KAYAK''s underlying meta-search infrastructure.'
