description: Instadapp is a decentralized finance (DeFi) middleware company that scales DeFi for users and developers through non-custodial Smart Accounts. Its core DSA (DeFi Smart Accounts / DeFi Smart Layer) protocol aggregates lending, DEX, and yield protocols behind a single upgradable smart-contract layer, driven by a composable cast() function, with connectors and resolvers for protocols such as Aave, Compound, MakerDAO, and Uniswap. Instadapp also builds Fluid (a capital-efficient lending and DEX protocol), Avocado (a smart-contract wallet with account, gas, and smart-contract execution), and Instadapp Lite (simplified DeFi vaults). It supports Ethereum, Polygon, Optimism, Arbitrum, Base, Avalanche, BNB Chain, Fantom, Gnosis, Scroll, and Polygon zkEVM. The developer surface is on-chain smart contracts plus first-party JavaScript and TypeScript SDKs published to npm; there is no first-party REST API. Instadapp is backed by Pantera Capital.
