description: Instadapp is a decentralized finance (DeFi) middleware company that scales DeFi for users and developers through non-custodial Smart Accounts. Its core DSA (DeFi Smart Accounts / DeFi Smart Layer) protocol aggregates lending, DEX, and yield protocols behind a single upgradable smart-contract layer, driven by a composable cast() function, with connectors and resolvers for protocols such as Aave, Compound, MakerDAO, and Uniswap. It supports Ethereum, Polygon, Optimism, Arbitrum, Base, Avalanche, BNB Chain, Fantom, Gnosis, Scroll, and Polygon zkEVM. The developer surface is on-chain smart contracts plus first-party JavaScript and TypeScript SDKs published to npm; there is no first-party REST API. Instadapp is backed by Pantera Capital.
