description: Union Square Ventures (USV) is a thesis-driven, early-stage venture capital firm founded in 2003 by Fred Wilson and Brad Burnham and headquartered at 817 Broadway in New York City. USV invests at the edge of large markets being transformed by technological and societal pressures, historically backing large networks of engaged users that are differentiated through user experience and defensible through network effects, and more recently companies that broaden access to knowledge, capital, and well-being. The firm invests primarily at Seed and Series A across three strategies — a Core fund, a Climate fund, and an Opportunity (later-stage) fund — with check sizes typically ranging from roughly $1M at seed to the low-to-mid single-digit millions at Series A. USV runs a small, collegial partnership and focuses on social media, marketplaces, developer tools, learning/education, health, fintech, web3/decentralized protocols, energy, and climate. It reports roughly $1.9B in assets under
