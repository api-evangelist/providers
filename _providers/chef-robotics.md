description: Chef Robotics is a San Francisco, California physical-AI company building AI-enabled robots for high-mix food production. Its core product is an NSF-certified food-assembly robot that picks, scoops, and deposits a wide range of ingredients — fresh, cooked, IQF, sauces, leafy greens, mixed vegetables, and discrete piece items — into trays, burritos, wraps, pizza bases, and other containers on conveyor lines. The robots are powered by ChefOS, a proprietary Generalized Food Manipulation Model trained on tens of millions of real-world servings and tens of thousands of hours of production data, combining vision, decision-making, and robot control across a See / Think / Act stack with continuous Fleet Learning. Chef Robotics sells via a Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS) model that bundles hardware, software updates, 24/7 monitoring, and customer success. Deployed customers include Amy's Kitchen, Sunbasket, Cafe Spice, and Chef Bombay, with the fleet having produced 98+ million servings
  across the US, Canada, and Europe. The company is venture-backed (Kleiner Perkins, Avataar Ventures-led $43M Series A, Bloomberg Beta, Construct Capital) and serves frozen meals, fresh meals, co-manufacturing, D2C meal delivery, medically tailored meals, meatpacking, airline catering, and foodservice verticals. There is no public developer API, SDK, or open-source release for ChefOS — the platform is operated as a closed, fleet-managed service on Chef-owned hardware deployed inside customer facilities.
