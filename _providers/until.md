description: Until (Until Labs) is a biotechnology company building the medical infrastructure to pause biological time. The lab translates the fundamentals of cryobiology into engineered workflows across five dimensions — perfusion, cryoprotectants, cooling, rewarming, and organ health assessment — using vitrification (glass, not ice) to prevent ice formation during preservation and safe rewarming.
