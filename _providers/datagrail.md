description: DataGrail is a San Francisco-based data privacy platform founded in 2018 by Daniel Barber and Ignacio Zendejas that helps enterprises automate privacy operations and manage regulatory compliance risk across GDPR, CCPA, and other global privacy regimes. The platform combines a Live Data Map for real-time visibility into where personal data lives across an organization's SaaS and cloud footprint, a Request Manager for orchestrating Data Subject Requests (access, deletion, opt-out) across thousands of downstream systems, a Consent solution for cookie and tracker compliance with automated enforcement, a Risk Management module for privacy and AI risk assessments (PIA, DPIA, AI risk), and an embedded agentic AI named Vera that surfaces context-aware recommendations across the platform. DataGrail exposes a documented public REST API at /api/v2 covering authentication, assessments, privacy requests, opt-out requests, integrations, webhooks, integration files, and consent audit logs,

The company serves enterprise customers including Salesforce, HubSpot, Okta, Netgear, and Dexcom, advertises 2,000+ pre-built integrations across CRM, marketing, communications, HR, data, e-commerce, and support platforms, holds patents on its Live Data Map and Smart Verification technology, is SOC 2 and ISO certified, and is backed by Felicis Ventures, HubSpot Ventures, Okta Ventures, Basis Set Ventures, Cloud Apps Capital, Operator Collective, Thomson Reuters Ventures, and Third Point Ventures.
