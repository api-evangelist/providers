description: Kwest was a Berlin-based B2B SaaS platform for the operations behind renewable-energy installations. Founded by Robin Dechant, Marco Holst and Constantin Ehren, it gave solar, heat-pump and smart-meter installers a workflow platform covering project management, inspection documentation, automated report creation and incident management, plus integrations that synced installation data with the CRM, ERP and field-service tools those companies already ran (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Dynamics 365, Odoo, Xentral, weclapp, Zoho, Aurora Solar, Solytic, Praxedo, Zapier and others). Speedinvest invested in 2021. In 2024 Kwest was acquired by Kraken Technologies, the technology arm of Octopus Energy Group, which folded the Kwest workflow platform into the Kraken stack and opened its first development hub in Berlin around the seven-person team. Kwest published no public API or developer program, and the company no longer operates independently - its former getkwest.com domain has
