description: Kuaidian Reading (快点阅读) is a Chinese mobile reading community operated by Beijing Tiantong Interactive Technology Co., Ltd. (Sky Platanus Interactive, 天桐互动), positioning itself as China's first dialogue-based reading app. Instead of long-form web fiction, the platform publishes chat-style novels rendered as character dialogue, which it argues gives clearer characterization, tighter pacing and stronger reader immersion.
