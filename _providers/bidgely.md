description: Bidgely is a Mountain View, California energy analytics company that builds AI software for electric and gas utilities. Its flagship platform, UtilityAI, applies patented non-intrusive load monitoring (NILM) and disaggregation techniques to smart meter interval data to infer appliance-level energy usage — air conditioning, heat pumps, electric vehicles, pool pumps, water heaters and more — from the whole-home meter signal alone, without surveys, sub-meters, or smart plugs. The platform layers GenAI natural-language interfaces and Agentic AI workflows on top of disaggregation to support utility programs across demand-side management, customer engagement and high-bill alerts, load flexibility and time-of-use, electrification (EV and heat pump targeting), grid resiliency and DER planning, affordability and income-qualified outreach, gas efficiency, small and mid-sized business programs, and energy theft detection. Bidgely is sold to utilities as SaaS or deployable into customer
  AWS, Snowflake, Databricks, Azure, and Oracle environments, and exposes embeddable customer-facing widgets to utility digital channels. Bidgely has been deployed by more than 50 utilities across four continents — including FortisBC, Arizona Public Service, PacifiCorp, Avista, NV Energy, Nova Scotia Power, and SoCalGas — covering tens of millions of meters. Bidgely operates an internal REST API surface across regional endpoints (naapi.bidgely.com, api.eu.bidgely.com) for its own pilot deployments and embeddable widgets, but does not currently publish a public developer portal, OpenAPI specification, or self-service API key program; integration is conducted under utility contracts.
