Buzzer secured streaming rights covering the NBA, WNBA, NHL, PGA Tour and ATP Tour, and raised roughly $44 million in total, including a $20 million Series A in June 2021 co-led by Sapphire Sport and Canaan Partners alongside a large group of athlete and team-owner investors.
