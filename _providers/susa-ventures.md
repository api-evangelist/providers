description: Susa Ventures is a San Francisco-based early-stage venture capital firm founded in 2013 by Chad Byers, Leo Polovets, and Seth Berman on the belief that founders are the lifeblood of progress. Susa is a high-conviction, low-volume seed investor that concentrates on data-centric companies with durable competitive moats such as proprietary data, economies of scale, and network effects, and it deliberately caps each partner at a small handful of active companies so it can operate alongside founders in a late-stage, hands-on manner at the earliest stages. The firm invests primarily at pre-seed and seed (occasionally Series A), writing initial checks that typically range from roughly $1M to $5M across artificial intelligence, fintech, healthcare, logistics and supply chain, cybersecurity, developer tools and infrastructure, and consumer. Susa is known for a founder-first posture (it avoids forcing founders out) and for an unusually high seed-stage unicorn hit rate, with notable investments
  including Robinhood, Flexport, Andela, Newfront, Stord, Human Interest, Mux, Stedi, Viz.ai, and Casetext. The firm closed Fund V at $175M in 2025 and also runs the experimental Humba Ventures vehicle for frontier bets.
