description: Knowify is a New York-based construction management SaaS for trade contractors (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, painting, remodeling, concrete, excavation), covering the full job lifecycle from bidding through payment on a single platform. The product spans project execution (estimating, proposals and contracts, change orders, RFIs and submittals, scheduling, daily logs, document and photo management), service work (dispatch, invoicing, payments, field operations, asset and inventory management), and financial management (AIA billing, job costing, prevailing wage, purchase orders, expenses, WIP and reporting). Knowify is positioned as the official construction software partner of QuickBooks Online and offers deep two-way sync with QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Intuit Enterprise Suite, QuickBooks Payroll, QuickBooks Payments, and QuickBooks Time, alongside native integrations with CompanyCam, Billd, Lumber, eBacon, and AIA Contract Documents. Third-party automation is available
  via Zapier. Pricing starts at $99/month (Core), $249/month (Advanced), and custom Enterprise, with paid add-ons for Service Pro, Live Equipment Tracking (FleetSharp), AI Dashcams, and Tool Tracking. Knowify does not publish a public REST API, webhooks specification, SDK, CLI, or developer portal; programmatic access for third parties is limited to Zapier and the QuickBooks ecosystem.
