description: Kojo (formerly Agora, kojo.app) is a construction materials management and procurement platform built for specialty trade contractors — electrical, mechanical, concrete, drywall, flooring, and roofing. It connects the field, the purchasing team, the warehouse, the prefab shop, and accounting into a single materials workflow covering requesting, sourcing, purchasing, receiving, tool tracking, invoice matching, and payment.
