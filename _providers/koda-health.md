description: Koda Health is a Houston-based digital health company whose AI-enhanced patient decision support platform digitizes serious illness planning for health systems, health plans, and value-based care organizations. Its two solutions — Advance Care Planning (ACP) and Kidney Action Planning (KAP) for chronic kidney disease — walk patients through video-guided, interactive goals-of-care conversations and produce legally compliant advance directives, medical power of attorney, and POLST/DNR documents with digital signing and notarization across all 50 states. Completed plans are pushed into the provider's EMR through an integration Koda states meets HL7 FHIR standards, and can be shared with care teams, payors, and family. KodaCares adds human Patient Advocates for complex cases. Koda publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or SDKs; its API host (api.kodahealthcare.com) is a closed backend serving the app, and EMR interoperability is delivered through partner and health-system
  integrations rather than a self-serve public API.
