description: Moxion Power was a Richmond, California-based mobile battery energy storage (BESS) company founded in 2020 that designed, manufactured, and deployed large-scale mobile lithium-iron-phosphate battery units (the MP-75 and related platforms) intended to displace diesel generators across construction sites, film and television production, live events, utility work, and emergency response. The company built a 40,000 square-foot factory in Richmond, raised approximately $100 million in a Series B at a reported $1.5 billion valuation led by Tamarack Global with participation from Amazon's Climate Pledge Fund and Microsoft's Climate Innovation Fund, and signed a multi-year fleet partnership with Sunbelt Rentals. After delivering 400+ units it announced cessation of operations on July 29, 2024, laid off its entire workforce, and filed for Chapter 7 bankruptcy in the District of Delaware in August 2024 with approximately $201,980 in cash on hand and estimated liabilities of $100M–$500M.
  In September–October 2024, Viridi Parente acquired Moxion's Richmond facility, battery inventory, intellectual property, patents, and the Sunbelt Rentals relationship through the bankruptcy auction for approximately $7 million. Moxion Power had no public developer API, SDK, or open source release; its GitHub organization (github.com/moxion-power) contains zero public repositories.
