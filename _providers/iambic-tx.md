Both lines of work have been published at venues including NeurIPS 2025. The company''s internal pipeline is oncology-led: IAM1363, a brain-penetrant selective HER2 / HER2-mutant inhibitor, completed Phase 1/1b dose escalation in 2025 and is in dose optimization with clinical activity reported at ESMO 2025; IAM-K1 (KIF18A) and IAM-C1 (CDK2/4) are preclinical,
  alongside undisclosed neurology, gastrointestinal, and inflammation discovery programs. In February 2026 Iambic announced a discovery and technology collaboration with Takeda worth up to ~$1.7B plus royalties, following a $100M+ private financing in November 2025 and total disclosed funding of approximately $334M. The company has been named to the CNBC Disruptor 50 list in 2025 and 2026 and has signaled intent to access public markets, including via a SPAC. Iambic''s public GitHub footprint is limited to np-bench, a BSD-3 benchmark library for 3D biomolecular structure prediction; the underlying NeuralPLexer and Enchant models are proprietary and there is no public developer API, SDK, or commercial platform offering.'
