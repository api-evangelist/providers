description: 'ClusterK was a cloud cost-optimization startup founded in 2013 in Palo Alto, California by Dmitry Pushkarev. Its software let AWS users run production and batch workloads on Amazon EC2 Spot Instances at up to 90% savings versus on-demand pricing, by predicting spot-market prices and continuously rebalancing capacity across instance types. ClusterK shipped two products: ClusterK Balancer, which arbitraged the AWS Spot Market for cost savings, and ClusterK Cirrus, a cloud-native HPC grid scheduler. The company was backed by DCVC (Data Collective), Rally Ventures, and the Mayo Clinic Business Accelerator, raising roughly $1.2M. Amazon acquired ClusterK in April 2015 and absorbed the team and technology into AWS; the company no longer operates independently and publishes no public API, SDK, or developer surface.
