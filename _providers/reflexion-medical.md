The company designs, manufactures and commercializes the RefleXion X1 radiotherapy platform and SCINTIX biology-guided radiotherapy, which uses onboard PET detectors to sense emissions from an injected radiopharmaceutical and autonomously direct external-beam radiotherapy delivery with sub-second latency. The RefleXion X1 received FDA clearance for SRS, SBRT and IMRT in 2020, and SCINTIX therapy was FDA cleared in February 2023 for treating tumors in the lung or bone arising from primary and metastatic cancer. RefleXion has raised more than $500 million from investors including TPG's The Rise Fund, KCK Group, PSP Investments, Sofinnova, Pfizer Ventures and Johnson & Johnson Innovation.
