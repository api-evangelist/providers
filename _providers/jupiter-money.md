description: Jupiter Money is a Bengaluru-based Indian digital banking app (neobank) operated by Amica Financial Technologies Private Limited. Founded in 2019 by Jitendra Gupta, Jupiter delivers a consumer "money app" experience that bundles savings and salary accounts, UPI payments, co-branded credit cards, personal loans, mutual funds, digital gold, fixed and recurring deposits, insurance, and a Jewels rewards program into a single mobile application. Jupiter is itself not a bank — it operates as a fintech marketplace and Loan Service Provider on top of regulated partners. Federal Bank is the primary banking partner for savings, salary, and deposit products, with CSB Bank powering the Edge+ and Edge RuPay credit cards and Federal Bank the Edge Federal VISA card. Lending is provided through partners including Amica Finance Private Limited and Kisetsu Saison Finance, with mutual funds through AIAPL and digital gold through MMTC-PAMP. The product is consumer facing and distributed via the
  Android and iOS apps; Jupiter does not publish a public developer API, SDK, OpenAPI specification, or developer portal. All integrations with banks, NBFCs, NPCI/UPI rails, card networks, and depositories are private business-to-business arrangements rather than an open API program.
