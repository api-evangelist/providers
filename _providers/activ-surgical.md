Its flagship product, the ActivSight Intelligent Light imaging module, attaches to existing laparoscopic and robotic surgical systems and renders augmented-reality overlays on a standard monitor, letting surgeons see physiology the naked eye cannot. ActivPerfusion delivers dye-free, real-time blood-perfusion imaging using laser speckle contrast imaging, while ActivICG supports conventional indocyanine green visualization of structures such as bile ducts. Activ Surgical performed the first autonomous robotic soft-tissue surgery in 2018, received FDA 510(k) clearance for ActivSight in April 2021 and CE Mark approval in December 2022, and sells on
