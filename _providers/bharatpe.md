description: BharatPe is a New Delhi-based fintech company founded in 2018 by Ashneer Grover and Shashvat Nakrani that provides digital payments and financial services to Indian small and medium businesses. Its merchant stack centers on a single interoperable UPI QR for payment acceptance across all UPI apps, augmented by BharatPe Speaker for voice transaction confirmations, BharatSwipe POS card machines for offline acceptance, BharatPe One (all-in-one POS), bill payments, and a co-branded credit card. On the financing side, BharatPe originates business loans for merchants through partner NBFCs, runs the InvestBharatPe / 12% Club investment platform, operates the PostPe buy-now-pay-later product, and is a co-promoter of Unity Small Finance Bank. The company reports more than ten million merchant partners across 450+ Indian cities and turned profitable in FY24 with roughly INR 1,700 crore in revenue. BharatPe is owned by Resilient Innovations Private Limited; it does not publish a public
  developer API, SDK, sandbox, or OpenAPI surface — its merchant and consumer products are delivered through native Android/iOS apps and an enterprise sales channel for partners.
