description: Monarch (Monarch Money) is a modern personal finance and money-management app that brings a household's accounts, budgets, investments, and goals into one shared view. It aggregates connected bank, card, loan, and investment accounts, automatically categorizes transactions, and lets couples, families, and financial professionals collaborate on budgeting and net-worth tracking at no extra cost. Monarch is a direct-to-consumer subscription product ($99/year) and does not publish an official public developer API — account connectivity is handled by third-party aggregators and the app is driven by a private internal GraphQL backend (api.monarch.com/graphql, authenticated).
