description: Kabbage was an Atlanta-based financial technology company (founded 2009) that pioneered fully automated, data-driven cash-flow lending to small businesses, offering flexible lines of credit, online bill payment, cash-flow visualization, and business checking. It exposed a B2B "Kabbage Platform" lending-as-a-service API (formerly documented at kabbageplatform.com) and was backed by the SoftBank Vision Fund. American Express announced its acquisition of substantially all of Kabbage in August 2020; the products were folded into American Express Business Blueprint and the standalone Kabbage developer platform was discontinued. As of this enrichment pass no independent Kabbage public API, developer portal, or specification is reachable — kabbage.com redirects to americanexpress.com/business/blueprint and the former developer hosts no longer resolve.
