description: Lollipuff is a luxury goods authentication service that has been authenticating high-end designer apparel and accessories professionally since 2010. Led by CEO Fei Deyle, the company uses a patent-pending process combining human expert authenticators with software to determine the authenticity of pre-owned designer items from submitted photographs, issuing an online certificate with a unique, shareable URL that owners can post alongside listings on sites like eBay. Lollipuff (YC W13) originally operated as a peer-to-peer online auction marketplace that pre-screened every listing for authenticity; the marketplace closed in 2020 as payment-processor fee structures changed, and the company now focuses on its photo-based authentication and certificate-lookup service, which many consignment and resale businesses rely on for their most challenging authentications. It is a consumer-facing web service with no publicly documented developer API.
