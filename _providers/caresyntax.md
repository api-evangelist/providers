description: Caresyntax is a surgical intelligence company that builds a vendor-neutral, enterprise-scale data platform for the operating room. Founded in 2013 in Berlin and now dual-headquartered in Berlin and San Francisco, it captures video, audio, image, device, EMR, financial and outcomes data from inside and around the OR and applies AI analytics to it — delivering pre-operative workflow optimization, real-time intraoperative decision support, and post-operative clinical safety, quality and utilization insights. The platform pairs the PRIME365 vendor-neutral OR / medical-device integration layer with the qvident surgical performance-management and analytics suite, and is deployed in more than 4,000 operating rooms worldwide. Caresyntax sells to hospitals and surgery centers, MedTech manufacturers and insurers, and integrates with EMR, PACS and scheduling systems. It operates an API gateway at api.caresyntax.com, but publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, SDKs or machine-readable
