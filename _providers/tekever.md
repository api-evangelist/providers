Founded in 2001, the company specialises in AI-driven autonomy for civil and defence missions, with its core business centred on long-endurance Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) used for Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR), maritime surveillance, and electronic warfare. Its flagship platforms include the AR3, a configurable medium-range tactical UAS (designated StormShroud in service with the UK Royal Air Force), the AR5, a SATCOM-enabled long-endurance maritime and land ISR aircraft, and the ARX, a next-generation system positioned as the company's game changer. These platforms are paired with ATLAS, Tekever's real-time intelligence-as-a-service platform that fuses sensor and satellite
  data into actionable mission intelligence. Tekever drones are operated by the UK Ministry of Defence, the British Army, the Armed Forces of Ukraine (with the UK having committed approximately £270 million in Tekever UAVs for Ukraine since the start of the Russian invasion), the European Maritime Safety Agency under a €30 million contract, the French Ministry of Armed Forces (CNES/DGA), Portugal, and NATO. The company also develops satellite technology and Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) capabilities to extend its intelligence services beyond the airborne domain. Tekever's revenue model is built on government and defence contracts, platform sales, and managed ISR-as-a-service offerings; it does not publish a public developer API, SDK, or open-source release, and engagement with the company is conducted through direct sales, government procurement, and partnership channels.
