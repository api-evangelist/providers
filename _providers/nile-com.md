description: Nile is a San Jose, California enterprise networking company delivering an AI-native Secure Network as a Service (NaaS). Founded in 2018 by former Cisco executives Pankaj Patel and John Chambers (chairman), Nile replaces traditional campus and branch LAN/Wi-Fi hardware-and-license procurement with a subscription-based, outcome-guaranteed wired and wireless network stack delivered as a service. The Nile Access Service combines its purpose-built Zero Trust Fabric (switches, access points, gateways, and sensors) with cloud-based autonomous operations, the Nile Services Cloud, Nile AI, Nile Nav, and Nile Digital Twin to plan, deploy, monitor, and self-heal enterprise networks. The platform emphasizes built-in Zero Trust segmentation, continuous performance guarantees (coverage, capacity, availability), and consumption-based pricing — marketed against 60%+ breach reduction, 99%+ ticket reduction, ~3x faster change management, and ~40% TCO savings versus traditional enterprise networking.
  Nile does not publish a general-purpose public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDK, CLI, or Terraform provider; its programmable surface is limited to a closed admin/portal API and outbound webhooks used for documented integrations with ServiceNow (ticket and alert webhooks), Splunk HEC, CrowdStrike, Zscaler ZIA, Microsoft Entra (SCIM), Microsoft Intune, Microsoft Global Secure Access, Palo Alto Networks, Forescout (device inventory API), SecureW2, Aruba ClearPass, VeloCloud, Graphiant, Hamina, and Ekahau. Nile is available through the AWS Marketplace as a SaaS subscription.
