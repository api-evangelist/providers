description: Barefoot Networks was a computer-networking company founded in 2013 in Santa Clara, California, that designed and produced programmable network-switch silicon, systems, and software. Its flagship Tofino Intelligent Fabric Processor was the first end-user-programmable Ethernet switch ASIC, built on the Protocol Independent Switch Architecture (PISA) and programmed with the P4 language at line rates up to 12.8 Tbit/s. Developer tooling spanned the P4 Studio SDK, the Barefoot Runtime Interface (BRI) control-plane API, and the Tofino Native Architecture (TNA). Intel acquired Barefoot Networks in June 2019, folding the technology into Intel Tofino / Intel Intelligent Fabric Processors; the barefootnetworks.com corporate site now redirects into Intel, and the surviving open documentation lives in the barefootnetworks GitHub organization (notably the Open-Tofino repository publishing the public TNA and P4 architecture docs).
