description: 'Likeplum (likeplum.com) was an online expert-knowledge and task marketplace that billed itself as "expert knowledge and skills at your fingertips" and invited users to "ask experts to answer your questions and hire skilled engineers, programmers, designers, writers, marketers and more on the world''s largest information trading marketplace." Members posted either a free-to-ask question or a paid task, received answers and completed work from the site''s expert pool, and paid only after approving the result under a post-pay model, with optional fully private posts and card or PayPal settlement. Its busiest categories were writing, programming, graphic design, and marketing and SEO. By late 2014 the company had folded the brand into the "Createpool" network, splitting its four most popular categories into dedicated sites — hackerpool.com for programming tasks, studypool.com for academic and homework Q&A, mechanicpool.com for auto repair, and counselpool.com for legal questions
  — and migrated every likeplum account, question, answer, and profile across. Only the academic property survives today: likeplum.com now 301-redirects to www.studypool.com, while createpool.com fails TLS negotiation and hackerpool.com, mechanicpool.com, and counselpool.com no longer resolve.
