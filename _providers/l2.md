description: 'L2 Inc was a subscription research and business intelligence firm founded in New York City in 2010 by NYU Stern marketing professor Scott Galloway and co-founder Maureen Mullen. L2 benchmarked the digital competence of consumer brands, publishing more than 25 Digital IQ Index reports each year covering over 2,200 brands across fashion, luxury, retail, beauty, financial services, hospitality and consumer packaged goods. Its research scored brands on site experience, e-commerce, digital marketing, social media and mobile execution, and was sold as a subscription to CMOs and senior marketing leaders. The firm raised roughly $16.5 million in venture funding, including backing from General Catalyst (Larry Bohn and Paul Sagan) from 2014. Gartner acquired L2 in March 2017, when it had about 150 employees across New York and London, and folded the Digital IQ research into Gartner for Marketers. L2 never operated a public developer program or API: its research was delivered through
