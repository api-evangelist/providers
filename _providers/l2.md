L2 benchmarked the digital competence of consumer brands, publishing more than 25 Digital IQ Index reports each year covering over 2,200 brands across fashion, luxury, retail, beauty, financial services, hospitality and consumer packaged goods. Gartner acquired L2 in March 2017, when it had about 150 employees across New York and London, and folded the Digital IQ research into Gartner for Marketers.
