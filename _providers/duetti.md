description: Duetti is a New York-headquartered music finance and catalog services company founded in 2022 by Lior Tibon and Christopher Nolte that buys music rights directly from independent artists, songwriters and creators. Artists earning at least $2,000 a year from tracks at least six months old can sell masters, publishing shares or a percentage of their royalties for an upfront cash payment — no debt, interest or recoupment — while keeping whatever they do not sell. Alongside the catalog purchases Duetti provides distribution, royalty tracking and claiming, playlisting across a network of owned Spotify playlists, sync placement, paid advertising, influencer and UGC campaigns, and a commissioned remix program. The company has raised more than $435 million, works with 1,100+ music creators, and publishes the Music Economics Report and the Billboard Music Finance Index. Duetti operates artist-facing web applications (DUO onboarding, statements vault, sync portal) but publishes no public
