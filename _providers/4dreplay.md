description: 4DReplay is a media technology company that builds multi-camera, time-slice ("bullet-time") video capture and replay systems for live sports, entertainment and events. Arrays of roughly 50 to 120 synchronized cameras — locked to within 1/10,000 of a second — are processed in real time to produce any-angle replay highlights within seconds of a play and deliver them to broadcast, mobile and OTT audiences. The product line spans 4DReplay (premium broadcast instant replay), 4DReplay+ / 4DLive (5G-based interactive multi-view live streaming with speed, angle and tracking overlays), and 4Dist, a browser-based interactive OTT platform that adds automated AI motion and swing analysis for academies, coaches and players. Founded in 2012 by Hongsu Jung and headquartered in San Mateo, California, with offices in Seoul and Tokyo, the company has worked with ESPN, NBC, CBS, FOX Sports, the NBA, MLB, NHL, the PGA Championship and the Olympics. As of August 2026 4DReplay publishes no public
