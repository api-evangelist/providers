description: 'LanzaJet is a sustainable fuels technology company, headquartered at 520 Lake Cook Road, Deerfield, Illinois, that produces and licenses sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) and renewable diesel. Founded in 2020 as a spin-out of LanzaTech with Suncor and Mitsui, and later backed by Shell, British Airways, Southwest Airlines, Microsoft''s Climate Innovation Fund, Breakthrough Energy, Groupe ADP, MUFG and All Nippon Airways, the company commercializes a patented Alcohol-to-Jet (ATJ) process that converts ethanol into synthetic paraffinic kerosene through a continuous catalytic reaction, reaching roughly 90% hydrocarbon selectivity to jet fuel and converting about 98% of the ethanol carbon into salable product. The ATJ pathway was approved by ASTM International in 2018. LanzaJet Freedom Pines Fuels in Soperton, Georgia — opened in 2024 — is the world''s first commercial ethanol-to-jet SAF plant, producing roughly 10 million gallons of SAF and renewable diesel a year, and the company
