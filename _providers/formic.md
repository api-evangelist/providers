description: Formic is a Chicago, Illinois-based Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS) company founded in 2020 by Saman Farid that delivers full-service industrial automation to American manufacturers on a pay-per-hour, fixed monthly subscription model with no capital expenditure. Formic specializes in end-of-line manufacturing operations — palletizing, case packing, and robotic pallet wrapping — and pairs deployed robots with a 24/7 remote monitoring, support, and field-service operation. The company's software stack includes Formic Core, an embedded operating system that abstracts motion, vision, sensing, and real-time coordination across heterogeneous industrial robot hardware (Fanuc, ABB, Kuka, Doosan) behind unified control interfaces; Formic Cortex; the Colony 2.0 fleet dashboard; FAST configuration tools; and Formic Production Intelligence (FPI), a manufacturer-facing analytics surface. Formic has raised more than $86M across rounds led by Lux Capital, Blackhorn Ventures, and other investors,
  and works through a network of 100+ system-integrator partners with 500+ systems deployed. Formic's revenue model is the RaaS subscription itself; there is no public developer API, no published SDKs, and no public customer-portal API documentation. Formic Core's "unified APIs" are internal abstractions used by Formic engineers and integrator partners to drive robot hardware — they are not exposed to manufacturers or third-party developers.
