description: Kymera Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company headquartered at 500 North Beacon Street in Watertown, Massachusetts, pioneering targeted protein degradation as a way to build oral small-molecule medicines against targets that conventional inhibitors cannot reach. Rather than blocking a protein's activity, Kymera's degraders recruit the body's own protein-disposal machinery so that a binding event alone is sufficient to eliminate the disease-driving protein, an approach the company describes as a disease-agnostic modality. Its wholly-owned pipeline is led by KT-621, a once-daily oral STAT6 degrader in Phase 2 for Type 2 inflammatory diseases including atopic dermatitis, asthma, COPD and chronic spontaneous urticaria, and KT-579, a first-in-class oral IRF5 degrader in Phase 1 for lupus, Sjogren's syndrome and rheumatoid arthritis. Partnered programs include the oral IRAK4 degrader KT-485/SAR447971 with Sanofi and the CDK2 molecular glue degrader KT-200 with
