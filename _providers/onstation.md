description: OnStation is a digital stationing platform for road and highway construction projects, replacing paper station logs and manual chaining with GPS-based field documentation. Its iOS and Android mobile app lets crews, inspectors, and project managers identify exact station locations, drop and share flags, capture timestamped photos and notes, and complete forms in the field without extra hardware. A cloud Data Portal aggregates that field documentation into searchable, exportable DOT-ready reports for compliance and dispute resolution, while Active Worksite alerts drivers through Waze and OEM systems when crews are working. OnStation offers pre-built integrations with construction and survey systems including HCSS HeavyJob, Trimble and Bad Elf GNSS receivers, TransTech density gauges, FleetWatcher e-ticketing, and ESRI GIS, but does not currently publish a public developer API.
