Its Data Management API ingests users, locations, meters, consumption readings and tariff price formulas — synchronously or as asynchronous NDJSON bulk jobs — and its Insights API returns presentation-ready analytics on top of that data: consumption and cost by day, week, month and year, CO2 footprint, appliance-level disaggregation (NILM), day-ahead market prices, peak power and dynamic capacity, tariff comparison, PV production, forecasting, anomaly detection, similar-homes benchmarking, budgets and personalised advice.
