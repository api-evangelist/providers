description: Geneoscopy is a commercial-stage molecular diagnostics company headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri, focused on gastrointestinal health. Its platform analyzes stool-derived eukaryotic RNA (seRNA) biomarkers, a proprietary approach the company applies to colorectal cancer screening, inflammatory bowel disease and other gastrointestinal conditions. Its lead product, ColoSense, is a noninvasive multi-target stool RNA (mt-sRNA) colorectal cancer screening test approved by the FDA in May 2024 for adults aged 45 and older at average risk; it evaluates eight colorectal neoplasia-associated RNA markers together with occult hemoglobin, and has since been added to the American Cancer Society and NCCN colorectal cancer screening guidelines. Geneoscopy operates a CLIA-certified (26D2253457) and CAP-accredited clinical laboratory with permits in New York, California, Maryland, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island and the District of Columbia, and partners with biopharma and research organizations
  on stool-biomarker diagnostics and prognostics. Geneoscopy is a diagnostics and clinical-laboratory company; as of this profile it publishes no public developer API, SDK, developer portal, or machine-readable API contract.
