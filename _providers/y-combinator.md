YC runs a three-month program four times a year (winter, spring, summer, fall), each batch typically funding 250-300 early-stage startups. Its standard deal invests $500,000 in every company: $125,000 for 7% of the company on a post-money SAFE, plus $375,000 on an uncapped SAFE with a Most Favored Nation (MFN) provision.
