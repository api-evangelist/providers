description: 'Équipe Communications Corporation was an Acton, Massachusetts telecommunications equipment startup established in 1999 by a group of telecom industry veterans. It designed, manufactured, and sold data network switching equipment for large-scale facilities-based carriers and service providers — Incumbent Local Exchange Carriers (ILECs), Inter Exchange Carriers (IXCs), and international carriers. Its product line comprised the Équipe 3200 Multiservice ATM/MPLS Core Data Switch, Évail System Software, ÉvailNet Manager, and Élite LifeCycle service and support. The positioning was to let carriers scale an existing ATM core network while offering a gradual, low-risk transition path to MPLS. The company raised a $40 million third round in March 2002, was awarded the first of 74 patents in February 2002, earned ISO 9001:2000 certification from TÜV Management Service in February 2002, signed a sales agent/reseller agreement with Ciena in June 2002 and a services master agreement with
  Fujitsu in August 2002, and achieved NEBS certification for the É3200 in January 2003. Équipe was a carrier-hardware vendor of the pre-API era: it never published a public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable interface. This profile is retained as a historical record of the Battery Ventures portfolio and is not an active API provider.
