description: Click Therapeutics, Inc. is a New York-headquartered digital medicine company founded in 2012 by David Benshoof Klein that develops, validates, and commercializes software as prescription medical treatments — the category it named Digital Therapeutics. Its AI-enabled platform targets diseases with an underlying neurological or behavioral component through cognitive and neurobehavioral mechanisms, delivered as smartphone software used independently or alongside biomedical therapy (Software-Enhanced Drugs). Authorized and commercial programs include CT-152 (Rejoyn, major depressive disorder, developed with Otsuka), CT-132 (episodic migraine) and AspyreRx (type 2 diabetes), with a pipeline spanning schizophrenia (CT-155 with Boehringer Ingelheim, CT-156), opioid use disorder, multiple sclerosis, atopic dermatitis, obesity, oncology, insomnia and acute coronary syndrome. Click publishes no public developer program, API, or SDK — its software is distributed as regulated prescription
  therapeutics, not as a platform.
