Co-founded by Jeff Cardenas (CEO), Dr. Nick Paine (CTO), and Dr. Luis Sentis (scientific advisor), the company designs and manufactures Apollo, a general-purpose humanoid robot standing 5'8", weighing 160 lbs, capable of lifting 55 lbs, and running for roughly 4 hours on a swappable battery. Apollo targets warehouse, manufacturing, and logistics applications including trailer unloading, case picking, palletization, machine tending, and workcell delivery. The company
