description: Kojo Technologies (formerly Agora Systems) builds construction materials management software for trade contractors — electrical, mechanical, plumbing, concrete and specialty trades. The Kojo platform centralizes procurement and purchasing, field requisitions from a mobile app, warehouse and inventory management, tool tracking, prefab workflows, project operations, and AI-assisted accounts payable invoice capture and matching. Kojo connects contractors to more than two hundred material suppliers and integrates with fifteen-plus construction accounting and project management systems including Procore, Autodesk, Sage, Acumatica, CMiC, Vista, Spectrum, FOUNDATION and QuickBooks. Kojo is backed by Battery Ventures. As of this profile Kojo publishes no public developer portal, API reference, or open API program — integration is delivered through its own supplier and ERP connectors rather than a self-serve developer API.
