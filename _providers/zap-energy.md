description: Zap Energy is a Seattle, Washington fusion energy company pursuing commercial fusion power via the sheared-flow-stabilized Z-pinch — a compact, magnet-free pulsed-power approach in which a flowing column of deuterium plasma is compressed (pinched) to fusion conditions inside a roughly two-meter metal tube, with shear in the axial flow stabilizing the pinch in place of the heavy superconducting magnets used by tokamak and stellarator designs. Founded in 2017 by Benj Conway, Brian A. Nelson, and Uri Shumlak as a spinout of University of Washington and Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory research, the company has developed a lineage of devices — ZaP, ZaP-HD, FuZE, FuZE-Q, and FuZE-3 — culminating in 2024 with Century, a 100-kW-scale repetitive pulsed-power test platform with liquid-metal walls intended to demonstrate plant-relevant technologies. In November 2025 FuZE-3 reported plasma electron pressures of 830 MPa (1.6 GPa total), the highest pressure measured in any sheared-flow-stabilized
  Z-pinch and a marker on the path to scientific energy gain (Q>1). Zap Energy was selected for the U.S. Department of Energy's Milestone-Based Fusion Development Program in May 2023 and received DOE approval of the preconceptual design of its Fusion Pilot Plant. The company has raised over $325 million across Series A–D from Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Lowercarbon Capital, Energy Impact Partners, Chevron Technology Ventures, Shell Ventures, DCVC, Valor Equity Partners, and Addition. As of April 2026, Zabrina Johal serves as CEO with Conway as President, and the company has expanded its mandate to also pursue small modular fission alongside its fusion roadmap. Zap Energy has no public developer API, SDK, GitHub organization, or open-source release; the technical surface is published as peer-reviewed papers and DOE milestone reports.
