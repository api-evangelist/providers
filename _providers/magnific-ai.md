description: Magnific is a Spanish AI image enhancement startup, founded in 2023 by Javi Lopez and Emilio Nicolas, best known for its AI image upscaler and enhancer that hallucinates believable new detail at higher resolutions. The product line expanded from the original Upscaler into Relight (AI-driven scene relighting), Structure (compose-from-sketch), Style Transfer, Mystic (text-to-image), and Fluffy (consistent characters). Magnific was acquired by Freepik in May 2024 and continues to operate under the Magnific brand while powering Freepik's image enhancement surface. There is no publicly documented developer API for Magnific itself; access is via the web application at magnific.ai (now magnific.com) and through the Freepik AI suite, where related image generation and upscaling capabilities are exposed via the Freepik API. Customer support is handled through an Intercom-backed help center and the founders maintain a high-visibility presence on X/Twitter.
features:
- Upscaler — AI image upscaler that hallucinates believable new detail at higher resolutions (the original Magnific product).
- Relight — re-lights any image with AI, driven by text prompts, reference images, or 3D lighting setups.
- Structure — generates new images that respect the composition of a sketch, edge map, or reference image.
- Style Transfer — transfers the visual style of one image onto another while preserving structure.
- Mystic — Magnific's text-to-image generation model focused on photographic realism and creative control.
- Fluffy — character consistency model for keeping subjects recognizable across multiple generations.
- Creativity, HDR, and Resemblance sliders for fine-grained control over upscaling output.
- Web application with credit-based pricing tiers; no public developer API for Magnific itself.
- Freepik integration — Magnific capabilities are accessible through Freepik's broader AI creative suite following the 2024 acquisition.
