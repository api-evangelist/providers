description: Aepona was a telecommunications software company founded in 1999 and headquartered in Belfast, Northern Ireland, with additional offices in Dublin, the United States, and Sri Lanka. Its Network-as-a-Service platform let mobile network operators and cloud service providers expose carrier network capabilities — messaging, voice, location, identity, and carrier billing — to third-party developers through common-language REST APIs, and merchandize them through multi-sided business models.
