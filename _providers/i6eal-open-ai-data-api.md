Backed by a live OpenAPI 3.0.3 contract with 83 GET operations, a DCAT JSON-LD catalogue of 16 datasets and 82 distributions, a provider-published llms.txt, and per-dataset Atom change feeds. No authentication, no registration, no published rate limit; CORS is open and responses carry ETag validators. The i6eal compilation is CC BY 4.0; underlying official records retain their source-specific rights.
