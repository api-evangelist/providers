description: Bouffalo Lab (博流智能) is a fabless chip design company founded in 2016 and headquartered in Nanjing, China, focused on ultra-low-power AIoT (AI + IoT) and edge-computing system chips. Its product line centers on RISC-V based microcontrollers with integrated wireless connectivity — the BL602/BL604 (Wi-Fi 2.4G + BLE 5.0), BL702/BL704/BL706 (Zigbee 3.0 + BLE 5.0), BL606P (quad-mode dual-core Wi-Fi/BT/BLE/Zigbee), and the BL616/BL618 and BL808 families — combining wireless radios, audio/video processing, and on-device AI algorithms. The company ships the open-source BouffaloSDK (a merge of bl_mcu_sdk and bl_iot_sdk) under Apache 2.0 through its public GitHub organization, along with hardware abstraction drivers, Matter/Thread support, and NuttX RTOS ports. Bouffalo Lab is a hardware/firmware provider rather than a web-API provider; it exposes no public HTTP/REST developer API, so the enrichment pipeline captures its developer surface (GitHub SDK org) and domain security posture.
