description: BranchTrack is a browser-based authoring platform for building interactive branching scenarios and conversation simulations used in corporate learning and development. Instructional designers and L&D teams create decision-tree simulations with no coding or design skills, then deploy them inside existing LMS platforms or embed them into courses authored in Articulate Storyline, Rise, Adobe Captivate, Gomo, and Lectora. Simulations export as SCORM and xAPI (Tin Can) packages and can be shared as responsive, mobile-native experiences with learner analytics and completion tracking. BranchTrack is a portfolio company of Seedcamp.
