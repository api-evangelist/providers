description: 'Laserhub is a Stuttgart-based digital procurement platform for custom-manufactured metal and plastic parts, founded to replace the traditional RFQ cycle for sheet metal, tube and machined components with instant online quoting. Buyers register at app.laserhub.com, upload CAD files, and receive an automated feasibility check plus an instant quote covering laser cutting, plasma and flame cutting, tube laser cutting, bending, CNC turning and milling, weldments, and secondary processes such as threading, deburring, galvanising, powder coating and engraving. Laserhub is itself the contracting party and allocates each order algorithmically across a network of roughly 100 ISO 9001:2015 certified producers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, serving both SMEs and DAX40 manufacturers with framework agreements, a parts library, sample parts, inspection certificates and design guidelines. Laserhub publishes no public developer API, SDK, or API documentation as of this profile: api.laserhub.com
  is a private AWS API Gateway that rejects unauthenticated requests, and the only machine-adjacent ordering channel the company documents is an email-based ordering flow (anfrage@laserhub.com) that its own release notes describe as still in a test phase for a subset of customers.'
