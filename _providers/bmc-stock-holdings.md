The company was formed in December 2015 through the merger of Stock Building Supply and Building Materials Holding Corporation (BMHC) and traded on NASDAQ under the ticker BMCH, headquartered in Raleigh, North Carolina. On January 1, 2021, BMC Stock Holdings completed an all-stock "merger of equals" with Builders FirstSource (NYSE: BLDR), creating the nation''s largest supplier of structural building products to the professional homebuilding market. The combined company retained the Builders FirstSource name, BLDR ticker, and Irving, Texas headquarters. The merger had an enterprise value of approximately $2.5 billion and produced a company with more than $11 billion in annual sales, approximately 26,000 employees, and roughly 550
  distribution and manufacturing locations across more than 40 U.S. states. BMC Stock Holdings no longer exists as an independent entity. The legacy buildwithbmc.com domain redirects to bldr.com, and BMC product lines and branches have been absorbed into the Builders FirstSource network. This profile is retained as a historical Fortune 1000 record (BMC ranked #677 prior to the merger) and as a redirect to the successor profile at api-evangelist/builders-firstsource.
