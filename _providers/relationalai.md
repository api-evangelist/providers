Its core product, Rel, builds a semantic model from data in Snowflake and reasons over it — combining graph analytics, rules, predictive graph neural networks, and prescriptive optimization — to answer high-stakes business questions (supply chain, pricing, inventory, demand forecasting) with transparent, validatable reasoning steps.
