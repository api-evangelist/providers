Its lead programme, BTI-203, is a 600-patient, 75-site, 13-country randomized double-blind placebo-controlled Phase 2 study of rhu-pGSN in moderate-to-severe acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS), supported by a $20M BARDA DRIVe contract (75A50123C00067); a second Phase 2 programme targets inflammasome-driven decompression sickness
