description: Audi Connect is the connected-vehicle services platform from Audi AG, the Ingolstadt, Germany-based luxury automaker and member of the Volkswagen Group. Delivered to drivers through the embedded MMI infotainment system and the myAudi companion app for iOS and Android, Audi Connect spans remote vehicle control (lock/unlock, remote start, climate pre-conditioning, EV charge management), safety and security (Emergency Call/eCall, Audi Service Request, stolen-vehicle locator, geofencing, speed alerts), and in-car infotainment (real-time traffic, satellite map imagery, parking and fuel information, weather, news, natural voice recognition, Amazon Alexa, Amazon Music, Wi-Fi hotspot, and an in-vehicle app store with Disney+ and passenger gaming on supported models). Services are packaged as Audi connect CARE (included for ten years on new U.S. Audis), Audi connect NAV (navigation and live traffic), and Audi connect PLUS (premium streaming and Wi-Fi). For EVs and PHEVs, Audi Connect
  adds charging-station search, remote charge monitoring, and cabin pre-conditioning. Audi does not publish a public developer portal, OpenAPI specification, OAuth program, or partner SDK for myAudi/Audi Connect; programmatic access today is brokered by third-party connected-car aggregators (Smartcar, High Mobility, Enode, Mojio) under Audi's commercial agreements, and community projects reverse-engineer the mobile-app endpoints. Audi has publicly discussed an internal OpenAPI-driven API management strategy (with Nagarro) but the surface remains private to Audi, VW Group brands, and authorized partners.
