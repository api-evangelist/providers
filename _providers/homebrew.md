Named after the Homebrew Computer Club, the firm practices high-conviction, concentrated seed investing, taking board-level positions and acting as an extension of the founder's team. Roughly 90% of its investments are pre-seed and seed, with initial checks in the $100K-$500K range and the ability to follow on up to $25M+ in later rounds. Homebrew backs mission-driven founders working on large, urgent, and valuable problems across fintech, AI/ML, healthcare, climate, defense, logistics, real estate, and consumer, focused on companies operating in the Americas. After a decade of institutional funds that backed companies such as Chime, Plaid, Gusto, and Mercury, Homebrew announced in 2021 that it would stop raising outside LP
