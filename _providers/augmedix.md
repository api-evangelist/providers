description: Augmedix is a San Francisco-based health technology company providing ambient AI medical documentation (AI scribe) services that capture patient-clinician conversations and generate clinical notes so that physicians can focus on patient care instead of EHR data entry. Founded in 2012, the company went public on Nasdaq in 2021 (AUGX) and was acquired by Commure in October 2024, becoming part of Commure's clinician-workflow platform alongside HCA Healthcare-aligned tooling. Augmedix's product line spans three service tiers — Augmedix Go (self-service AI scribe), Augmedix Assist (AI drafting with human editing, coding, and referral-letter support, notes ready within an hour), and Augmedix Live (full-service dedicated medical documentation specialist before, during, and after the visit) — plus specialty variants for Emergency Medicine, oncology, and other specialties. The platform integrates with major EHRs including Epic, Oracle Cerner, athenahealth, Meditech, eClinicalWorks, NextGen,
  and iKnowMed, and is HIPAA-compliant and HITRUST-certified. Augmedix's GitHub presence consists of internal QA, scribe-app base, and ambient API automation repositories; there is no public developer API, SDK, or OpenAPI specification — integration with Augmedix is handled through enterprise EHR partnerships and the Commure clinician platform rather than a self-service developer surface.
