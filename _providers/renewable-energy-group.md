description: Renewable Energy Group (REG) is a North American producer of biodiesel and renewable diesel, founded with biodiesel operations dating to 1996 and formally established as a separate company in 2006, with headquarters in Ames, Iowa. REG operated as a publicly traded company on Nasdaq under the ticker REGI until February 28, 2022, when Chevron Corporation announced the acquisition of REG for approximately $3.15 billion; the deal closed on June 13, 2022, and REG now operates as a wholly owned subsidiary of Chevron under the Chevron Renewable Energy Group brand at chevron-reg.com. REG operates a fleet of biorefineries across the United States and Europe, producing biodiesel, renewable diesel, and related lower-carbon fuel products from a diversified mix of feedstocks including used cooking oil, inedible animal fats, distillers corn oil, and vegetable oils. As of this profile, neither REG nor the Chevron Renewable Energy Group subsidiary publishes a public consumer-facing developer
