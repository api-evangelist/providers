description: indoo.rs (indoo.rs GmbH) was a Vienna, Austria based Indoor Positioning System (IPS) startup founded in 2010 that built indoor mapping, localization, and routing technology plus a mobile SDK for developers and device manufacturers. Its solution combined Wi-Fi, BLE/iBeacon, sensor fusion, and dead-reckoning (SLAM-style) navigation to deliver real-time indoor positioning with accuracy down to roughly two meters. Backed early by Speedinvest, indoo.rs was acquired by Esri on February 28, 2019; its software became part of Esri's ArcGIS Indoors and its Vienna headquarters became the Esri R&D Center Vienna focused on IPS. The indoo.rs domain now redirects to the ArcGIS Indoors product, and the company no longer operates an independent public API or developer portal.
