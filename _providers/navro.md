description: Navro (formerly Paytrix) is a London-based payments curation platform that lets large businesses move money globally through a single contract and API integration. Its products span Navro Workforce (global payroll, contractor and pension payouts), Navro Life (cross-border pensions), Navro Digital (payments for digital platforms and enterprises) and Navro Capital (international fund flows).
