description: HistoSonics is a medical device company commercializing histotripsy, a non-invasive, non-thermal and non-ionizing sonic beam therapy that uses focused ultrasound to form a controlled bubble cloud which mechanically destroys and liquefies targeted tissue at a sub-cellular level. The underlying science originated with researchers at the University of Michigan, and the company maintains clinical partnerships with the University of Michigan and the University of Wisconsin.
