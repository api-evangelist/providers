description: Normative is a Stockholm, Sweden-based carbon accounting and ESG reporting software company helping enterprises measure, report, and reduce their greenhouse gas emissions across scopes 1, 2, and 3. Its platform combines a library of more than 349,000 region- and sector-specific emission factors with workflows for product carbon footprint (PCF), supply chain engagement, Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) target setting, Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) disclosure, and Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) reporting.
