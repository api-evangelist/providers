description: Jumbo (Jumbo Privacy) was a mobile-first consumer privacy and security assistant founded in 2019 by Pierre Valade, the co-founder of the Sunrise calendar app acquired by Microsoft. The iPhone/Android app helped everyday users take control of their online privacy by automatically hardening privacy settings across services like Google, Facebook, Twitter/X, Amazon, and LinkedIn, cleaning up old social posts, monitoring data breaches, and bundling identity-theft insurance. It was explicitly designed to process user data locally on-device with no server-side processing, so it never exposed a public developer API, SDK, or documented integration surface. Jumbo was backed by investors including Balderton Capital and Thrive Capital before being acqui-hired by cyber-insurer Coalition in 2023; the standalone consumer app was wound down and fully discontinued by 2024.
