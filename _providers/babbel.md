description: Babbel is a Berlin, Germany-based language learning company founded in 2007 by Thomas Holl, Markus Witte, Lorenz Heine, and Toine Diepstraten. It operates one of the world's first online language-learning platforms, offering structured, conversation-focused courses in 14 languages including Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Dutch, Turkish, Indonesian, and English. The Babbel product suite includes the core subscription Babbel App with interactive bite-sized lessons, speech recognition, and AI conversation partners; Babbel Live for online classes with certified teachers; Babbel for Business for enterprise language training; Babbel Podcasts and Babbel Magazine for supplemental content; and Babbel Videos on YouTube. The company has sold over 25 million subscriptions and reports that 92% of users improved their proficiency in two months, with effectiveness studies conducted by Michigan State University, Yale University, and
  CUNY. Babbel maintains an additional office in New York City and is privately held. Babbel's revenue model is direct-to-consumer subscriptions (monthly, annual, lifetime tiers) plus enterprise B2B licensing through Babbel for Business; there is no public developer API, SDK, or open-source learner content. The Babbel GitHub organization publishes internal infrastructure tooling (Terraform AWS modules, Puma plugins, OkHttp AWS signing, GitHub Actions) but no consumer-facing API.
