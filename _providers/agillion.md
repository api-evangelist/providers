description: Agillion, Inc. was an Austin, Texas web-based customer relationship management (CRM) company founded in 1998 by Steve Papermaster. It offered small and mid-sized businesses a hosted, continuously updated online database that consolidated customer information as businesses and their customers interacted, an early software-as-a-service CRM offering. The company raised roughly $40M from investors including Cisco, Goldman Sachs, and Morgan Stanley and is carried as a portfolio company of Insight Partners. Agillion has since been permanently closed; its agillion.com domain no longer serves a website and the company exposes no public developer portal, documentation, or API surface.
