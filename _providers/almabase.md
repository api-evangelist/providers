description: Almabase is an AI-powered alumni engagement and fundraising platform for higher education, K-12 schools, and nonprofit advancement teams. It sits on top of an institution's CRM to unify digital engagement programs (alumni directories, mentorship, affinity groups, job boards), event management, online giving and giving-day campaigns, and multichannel communications. Through its TrueSync bidirectional integrations with Blackbaud Raiser's Edge NXT, Blackbaud CRM, Salesforce, and Ellucian, Almabase keeps engagement, event, giving, and constituent data connected to the system of record so advancement teams can run campaigns and report on impact with less manual work. Almabase does not publish a public developer REST API or OpenAPI; integration is delivered via managed CRM sync and a customer-facing developer portal.
