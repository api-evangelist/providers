description: Clinkle was a Silicon Valley mobile-payments startup founded in 2012 by Stanford student Lucas Duplan. In 2013 it raised roughly $30 million in what was then reported as the largest seed round in history, backed by Andreessen Horowitz, Peter Thiel, Marc Benioff, Accel's Jim Breyer, Richard Branson, and Intel Capital. The company promised a phone-to-phone payments technology but never shipped a viable product, launched a poorly received "Treats" debit-card app, cycled through multiple pivots and leadership turmoil, and lost its window to Venmo and Apple Pay. By 2016 Clinkle had quietly shut down with no official announcement. It is retained in the API Evangelist network as a defunct a16z portfolio lead; it publishes no live website, developer portal, documentation, or API surface today (clinkle.com resolves to a suspended-account page).
