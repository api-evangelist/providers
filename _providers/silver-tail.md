Its product collected and analyzed real-time web traffic to deliver web session intelligence and predictive analytics that detected fraud, account takeover, and abuse on banking and commerce websites. Backed by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) among others, the company raised roughly $20M across three rounds before being acquired by RSA, the security division of EMC, in 2012 and rebranded as RSA Web Threat Detection.
