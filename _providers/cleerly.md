Its software analyzes coronary CT angiography (CCTA) imaging to automatically quantify and characterize atherosclerotic plaque, measure stenosis severity, and stage disease, giving physicians a precise, personalized picture of a patient's heart-disease risk. The goal is to move heart-disease care from symptom-based testing toward direct measurement of plaque, reducing unnecessary invasive procedures while improving diagnostic accuracy. Cleerly operates as an enterprise clinical SaaS delivered to health systems, cardiologists, and imaging centers rather than a public developer API program; it publishes a marketing and clinical-evidence site plus a clinician-facing application (Cleerly Labs) and a public trust center.
