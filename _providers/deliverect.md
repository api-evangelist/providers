description: Deliverect is a global restaurant integration platform that connects delivery marketplaces, online ordering channels, POS systems, and store operations into a single hub serving over 80,000 locations across more than 50 markets. For developers and integration partners, Deliverect exposes a suite of REST APIs documented in its Developer Hub, including the POS, Commerce, Channel, Dispatch, Store, Pay, CRM, Loyalty, KDS, and Gift Cards APIs. The APIs let partners receive and manage orders and catalogues, build online ordering and kiosk channels, manage last-mile dispatch, process payments, manage customers and loyalty, and control stock, hours, and availability.
