description: Mojo Vision is a Saratoga, California semiconductor and display company building a "wafers-in, wafers-out" micro-LED platform. Founded in 2015, it originally developed the world's smallest, densest dynamic display for an AR smart contact lens, then pivoted in 2023 to commercializing micro-LED displays and optical interconnects. Its platform combines a 300mm silicon architecture with GaN-on-Silicon emitters, proprietary quantum dots and micro-lens arrays, and has demonstrated a monolithic RGB panel at 4µm pixel pitch (6,350 ppi). Target markets are XR and AI-enabled glasses, automotive heads-up displays, large-format displays, and high-density optical I/O for AI infrastructure. Partners include Marvell, DigiLens and CY Vision; investors include Khosla Ventures, NEA, Future Ventures, HP Tech Ventures, Amazon's Alexa Fund and Dolby Family Ventures.
