description: BrainSpec is a medical software company whose FDA-cleared, cloud-based platform BrainSpec Core makes MR spectroscopy (MRS) accessible to clinicians and researchers, turning standard MRI scanners into a non-invasive "virtual biopsy" that measures brain-chemistry metabolite concentrations. The web application ingests MRS data in DICOM, RDA, TWIX, SDAT and G-file formats across GE, Siemens and Philips 3T scanners, applies a linear-combination post-processing model, and interfaces directly with hospital PACS to pull companion images and push screenshots and reports back. BrainSpec is backed by DCVC and Techstars. As of this enrichment pass BrainSpec publishes no public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDK, or developer portal; integration is delivered as a managed clinical/PACS service.
