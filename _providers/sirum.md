description: SIRUM (Supporting Initiatives to Redistribute Unused Medicine) is a nonprofit and the country's largest redistributor of surplus prescription medication. It connects unused, in-date medicine from manufacturers, wholesalers, pharmacies, and nursing homes with patients who cannot afford their prescriptions, operating under drug-donation laws across the United States. SIRUM runs a logistics and matching platform that routes donated medication to community partners and individuals in need, aiming to close the gap for the tens of millions of Americans who skip prescribed medication because of cost.
