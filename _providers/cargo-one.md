description: cargo.one is a Berlin-headquartered digital freight platform that operates as an AI-native operating system for air and ocean freight logistics. The platform unifies freight forwarder operations by centralizing rates, partners, and systems on a single end-to-end surface shared between human teams and AI agents. For freight forwarders, cargo.one provides air e-booking with 76+ integrated airlines (300+ total network), ocean freight rate management with 10+ liner integrations, multimodal quoting across air, ocean, road, and local charges, a Rate Management System for buy/sell/internal rates, a branded 24/7 self-service Customer Portal, analytics dashboards, a 24/7 Global Network for cross-timezone quoting, and an API Suite with native TMS integrations for CargoWise, Cargospot, and Descartes. For carriers, cargo.one delivers a market-intelligence and customer-access platform spanning the end-to-end commercial cycle. AI workflows are offered in three deployment modes — Co-pilot
  (approval-based), Supervised (autonomous with escalation), and Autonomous — covering rate management, rate procurement, quoting, booking, and customer support automation, backed by retrieval-augmented generation and a cargo.one MCP Server for custom solutions. Notable customers include Lufthansa Cargo, Kuehne+Nagel, DSV, Maersk, CMA CGM, Hellmann, and Rhenus Logistics. cargo.one's API and TMS integrations are partner / enterprise offerings — there is no public self-serve OpenAPI specification, developer sandbox, or open-source SDK published outside of customer onboarding.
