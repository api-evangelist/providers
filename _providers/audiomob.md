description: Audiomob is an in-game and in-app audio advertising platform that lets mobile game and app publishers monetize their titles with non-interruptive, skippable and rewarded audio ads while advertisers reach engaged gaming audiences at scale. Publishers integrate through the Audiomob Unity plugin or native iOS and Android SDKs (plus a community React Native adapter), add their games to the Audiomob developer dashboard, and configure ad units, an Ad Availability API, and server-to-server webhooks for reward and event tracking. The platform is Open Measurement (IAB Tech Lab) compliant, supports consent management via OneTrust and Sourcepoint, exposes Impression Level Revenue Data (ILRD), and integrates with mediation partners such as AdMob and LevelPlay (MAX) and mobile measurement partners including Adjust, Singular and Tenjin. The current SDK line is SDK 10. Audiomob is backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners.
