Kuda offers retail banking accounts with zero or low fees, debit cards, instant transfers, bill payments, savings ("Spend+Save"), investments, overdrafts, and personal loans through its iOS, Android, and web apps, plus a Business banking suite covering expense accounts and cards, payroll, invoicing, softPOS and physical POS terminals, business loans, and CAC business registration. Kuda also exposes an Open API ("Kuda Open API") aimed at fintechs, marketplaces, and businesses that want to embed Nigerian banking primitives
