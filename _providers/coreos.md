description: 'CoreOS was a San Francisco-based container infrastructure company founded in 2013 that built a lightweight, automatically-updating Linux distribution (Container Linux, originally CoreOS Linux) and a family of now-foundational cloud-native open source projects: etcd (the distributed key-value store at the heart of Kubernetes), the Quay container registry, the Tectonic enterprise Kubernetes platform, the rkt container engine, flannel networking, Clair image-vulnerability scanning, and Ignition provisioning. Red Hat acquired CoreOS in January 2018 for approximately $250 million. Its technologies were folded into Red Hat''s portfolio — Container Linux became Red Hat CoreOS and the community Fedora CoreOS, Tectonic merged into OpenShift, Quay continues as Red Hat Quay, and etcd and rkt were donated to the Cloud Native Computing Foundation. The company no longer operates independently; coreos.com redirects to Red Hat OpenShift. The github.com/coreos organization remains active as
  the upstream home for Fedora CoreOS components such as Ignition, Butane, and fleetlock.'
