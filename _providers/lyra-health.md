The platform combines preventive self-care resources, coaching, evidence-based therapy, medication management, family and pediatric services, and specialized acute care delivered through a benefits-administration model sold to large employers and health plans rather than directly to consumers. Lyra's product surface includes the Lyra App for members, the Lyra Empower AI-powered care platform launched in 2025, Centers of Excellence for pediatric mental health and neurodiversity, a manager coaching program, and a clinical-grade AI guide. Customers include Salesforce, Lululemon, Morgan Stanley, Genentech, Starbucks, JE Dunn, VCA, and the School District of Philadelphia, and in March 2026 the company became Workday Wellness's
  preferred mental health partner. Lyra acquired pediatric and family mental health provider Bend Health in July 2025. Lyra is led by CEO Jennifer Schulz, with Jenny Gonsalves as Chief Product & Technology Officer, Alethea Varra as Chief Clinical Officer, and co-founder Bob Kocher, MD on the board. The company's revenue model is per-member-per- month employer contracts plus utilization-based clinical billing; Lyra does not publish a public developer portal, OpenAPI specification, SDK, CLI, or self-service API, and its only public GitHub repository is an internal fork of an unrelated infrastructure module. All employer, member, HRIS/eligibility, SSO, and EHR integrations are negotiated and delivered through the Lyra customer success and implementation teams rather than through a self-serve developer surface.
