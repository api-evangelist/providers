description: Kosmix Corporation was a Mountain View, California topic-discovery and search company operating from roughly 2005 to 2011. Rather than returning a ranked list of links, Kosmix automatically assembled a topic "home page for the web" for any subject, aggregating and categorizing content across health, science, food, sports, politics, travel, finance and other verticals.
