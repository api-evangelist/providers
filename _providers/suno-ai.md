description: Suno is a Cambridge, Massachusetts-based generative AI music company building a consumer platform that lets anyone create original songs from natural language prompts. The product spans text-to-song generation, vocal and instrumental tracks, style and genre control, stem splitting, audio upload and remixing, lyric writing, and Suno Studio for advanced multi-track editing and custom model tuning. The company runs proprietary foundation models for music generation, the latest being v5.5, and ships consumer subscription plans (Free, Pro, Premier) with commercial usage rights on paid tiers. Founded in 2021 by Mikey Shulman, Georg Kucsko, Martin Camacho, and Keenan Freyberg, Suno closed a $250M Series C in November 2025 at a $2.45B valuation led by Menlo Ventures with participation from NVentures, Hallwood Media, Lightspeed, and Matrix. The company has announced a partnership with Warner Music Group. Suno does not publish an official developer API or SDK; the consumer surface is
