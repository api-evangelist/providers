Party Protocol lets groups form a Party (a shared Ethereum account with members, an inventory, and governance), collectively crowdfund to acquire assets, govern shared holdings, and distribute value to members — all through audited Solidity smart contracts rather than a hosted HTTP API. PartyDAO also ships Party (party.app), a consumer application built on the protocol. The developer surface is entirely on-chain: smart contracts, deployed contract addresses, and technical documentation for integrating via web3 libraries and direct contract calls.
