description: Polycam is a cross-platform 3D scanning and capture company building consumer and professional tools for turning the physical world into high-fidelity 3D content. Its iOS, Android, and web apps support LiDAR-based space capture, photogrammetry, photosphere capture, AI-generated 3D models, and Gaussian Splat reconstructions, with export formats including glTF/GLB, OBJ, FBX, DAE, STL, USDZ, point clouds, and CAD-friendly files. Polycam serves freelancers, AEC and real-estate teams, VFX and game studios, e-commerce sellers, and enterprise operators with 2D/3D floor plans, measurement tools, shared team libraries, virtual walkthroughs, AI reports, custom branding, SSO, and TransformEngine for custom 3D file creation. Revenue is subscription based across Free, Basic ($30/mo or $150/yr), Business ($400/yr per user), and Enterprise tiers, with sales-led pricing for organizations at scale. Polycam exposes a developer mode in the iOS app that unlocks a raw-data export bundle (raw.glb
  mesh, keyframes, images, cameras, depth maps, confidence data, mesh_info.json) used by partners such as Vuforia, Immersal, and Unity/Unreal workflows, and ships an open-source Python toolkit (polyform) for converting raw captures to NeRF and other research formats. As of this profile there is no public REST API, OpenAPI specification, or developer portal — integrations happen through raw exports, partner pipelines, and the Polycam app surface itself.
