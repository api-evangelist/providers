description: Olive AI was a Columbus, Ohio healthcare automation company founded in 2012 by Sean Lane (originally as CrossChx, rebranded to Olive in 2017). The company built robotic process automation (RPA), AI, and workflow tooling aimed at the healthcare revenue cycle — including a "digital workforce" marketed as "Olive" that automated repetitive administrative tasks in hospitals and health systems. Olive's portfolio centered on clearinghouse and patient access automation, prior authorization automation, claims status checks, eligibility verification, and process mining for revenue-cycle optimization. At its peak Olive raised more than $900M and reached a $4B valuation, but a series of strategic missteps, layoffs, and cash burn forced an orderly wind-down. In late 2023 Olive sold its clearinghouse and patient access business to Waystar, and in early 2024 sold its prior authorization business to Humata Health, then ceased operations. The Olive AI brand is no longer active and there is no
  public developer API, SDK, or open-source release; surviving capabilities live on inside Waystar and Humata Health.
