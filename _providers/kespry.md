description: Kespry was an industrial drone and aerial-intelligence company founded in the San Francisco Bay Area that paired automated fixed-perimeter drones with a cloud analytics platform to turn aerial imagery into measurable data. Its product was used for stockpile and inventory volumetrics in mining and aggregates, construction-site progress monitoring and earthworks, and insurance roof inspection. Backed by Lightspeed Venture Partners, Kespry was acquired by Firmatek in 2020 and the brand has since been retired; the former developer and marketing domain kespry.com now issues a 301 redirect to firmatek.com.
