description: Kafene is a New York-based point-of-sale financing platform that lets retailers offer transparent lease-to-own (LTO) purchase options to prime and non-prime consumers. Founded in 2019 by Neal Desai and James Schuler, the company underwrites shoppers with an AI-driven risk model that pulls 20,000+ data points to return an approval — up to $5,000 — in seconds, then pays the merchant directly on delivery confirmation while the customer pays weekly or biweekly until ownership. Merchants originate applications from their in-store POS or by sending a text-to-apply link; consumers manage leases and payments in a self-service dashboard. Kafene raised a ~$31M Series B and reaches merchants through financing-waterfall networks such as Versatile Credit and LendPro. As of the 2026-08-01 enrichment probe Kafene publishes no public developer program, no OpenAPI/GraphQL/MCP contract, no SDKs, and no /.well-known discovery surface; its merchant and consumer surfaces are authenticated single-page
  web applications and integration is arranged through Kafene's partner team, not self-serve documentation.
