The company manufactures its battery at Form Factory 1, a 550,000-square-foot facility built on the former Weirton Steel mill site that employs roughly 400 people. Notable customers and partners include Georgia Power, Xcel Energy, Great River Energy, Dominion Energy, PG&E, New York Power Authority, and Crusoe Energy (a 12 GWh agreement to firm power for AI data centers).
