It separates the surveyor's professional fee for preparing or answering a schedule of dilapidations from the claim itself, which section 18(1) of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1927 caps at the diminution in value of the landlord's reversion. It publishes guides on survey costs, schedules of dilapidations, schedules of condition, the Dilapidations Protocol timetable and office dilapidations, citable datasets such as the Dilapidations Cost Index, and a quote form that passes matters to dilapidations surveyors for fee proposals.
