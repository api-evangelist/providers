description: ERGO NEXT Insurance (formerly NEXT Insurance) is a Palo Alto, California technology-first Property & Casualty insurer focused on the specific needs of U.S. small and medium-sized business owners. Founded in 2016, the company offers fully digital commercial coverage including General Liability, Workers' Compensation, Business Owner's Policy (BOP), Professional Liability / Errors & Omissions, Commercial Property, Commercial Auto, Tools & Equipment, Employment Practices Liability, Product Liability, and Liquor Liability — serving more than 750,000 customers across 1,300+ professions. In March 2025 Munich Re signed a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of NEXT Insurance at a valuation of $2.6 billion; ERGO Group AG (Munich Re's primary insurance arm) closed the full acquisition on July 1, 2025 and on January 15, 2026 the company rebranded as ERGO NEXT Insurance, becoming Munich Re/ERGO's foothold in the U.S. SMB market. ERGO NEXT's partner-facing surface is NEXT Connect, an embedded
  insurance platform that lets payroll, point-of-sale, accounting, e-commerce, payments, banking, and fintech platforms quote and bind small-business coverage from within their own product through low-code components and API calls. NEXT Connect aggregates 15+ carriers (including ERGO NEXT itself, Travelers, Liberty Mutual, Chubb, CNA, The Hartford) and is documented in the wild through embedded-payroll partners such as Check, Gusto Embedded, Intuit QuickBooks, Square, LegalZoom, and Amazon Business Prime, but ERGO NEXT does not publish a public developer portal, OpenAPI specification, or self-serve SDKs — partner onboarding is gated through a partnerships team. The company also offers a pay-as-you-go Workers' Comp product that integrates with partner payroll runs so premium is calculated against actual per-cycle W-2 wages.
