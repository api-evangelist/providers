description: Stride is a multichain liquid staking protocol built as a Cosmos SDK appchain (chain-id stride-1) and secured by Cosmos Hub interchain security. Users stake proof-of-stake assets such as ATOM, TIA, DYDX, OSMO, DYM, SAGA, and LUNA and receive liquid staking receipt tokens (stATOM, stTIA, and others) that keep accruing staking rewards while remaining usable across DeFi for trading, lending, collateral, and liquidity provision. Stride is IBC-connected and uses a Hyperlane bridge for cross-chain reach.
