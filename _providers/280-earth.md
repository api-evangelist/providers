description: 280 Earth is a direct air capture (DAC) company that permanently removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. Originally launched in 2018 inside X, the Alphabet moonshot factory, and spun out as an independent company in 2022, it is backed by Sergey Brin, Builders VC and a $50M Series B.
