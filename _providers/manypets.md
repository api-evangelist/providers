description: ManyPets (formerly Bought By Many, part of ManyGroup) is a digital-first pet insurance provider founded in 2012 by Steven Mendel and Guy Farley and headquartered in London, United Kingdom. The company sells lifetime dog, cat and multi-pet insurance direct to consumers in the UK, alongside a preventative flea/tick/worm plan, an online claims experience and unlimited 24/7 video vet consultations. Sister ManyGroup brands include ExoticDirect (exotic pet insurance) and VetBox (pet treatment subscriptions). ManyPets raised a $350M Series D led by EQT Growth in 2021 and has also operated in the United States and Sweden; the US book is transitioning to Odie. ManyPets does not publish a public developer portal or machine-readable API contract — its API is offered only through a gated partner programme (affiliate, widget, API partner, perk and custom solutions) operated at many-group.com/partners/.
