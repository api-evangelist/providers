description: Buildots is an AI-powered construction intelligence platform headquartered in Tel Aviv, Israel, with offices in London and the United States. The company combines 360-degree hardhat-mounted cameras with computer vision and machine learning models that automatically capture, locate, and classify on-site construction progress against the project BIM model and schedule, producing trade-level performance data, deviation and delay detection, and portfolio-wide analytics for general contractors, project teams, executives, and owners. Buildots reports that projects using the platform together with a performance-driven operating approach have reduced delays by up to 50%. The product family includes Buildots (the AI construction intelligence platform), Genda (workforce intelligence), and a fully managed Capture Services operation that handles routine site capture for customers. Verticals served include data centers, commercial, multifamily, healthcare, industrial, education, and airports.
  Notable customers shown on the website include Intel, Mortenson, JE Dunn, Turner, Wates, and STO. Buildots was founded in 2018 and is led by CEO and co-founder Roy Danon with co-founders Aviv Leibovici (CPO) and Yakir Sudry (CCO). Investors include Lightspeed Venture Partners, Future Energy Ventures, Viola Ventures, TLV Partners, OG Ventures, and Tencent. Buildots does not publish a public developer API, SDK, or developer portal; integrations with construction software (e.g., Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud) are arranged directly through the company.
