description: Levity (Levity AI GmbH, Berlin, Germany) is a Communication Intelligence platform for the global supply chain, delivering AI automation for freight operations on top of the systems logistics teams already run rather than replacing them. It turns the email and phone traffic that drives day-to-day freight work - quoting, order entry, track-and-trace, document processing and carrier communication, in any language - into structured, automated workflows with humans kept in the loop. The platform pairs inbox analytics (a control tower over millions of emails), an AI automation flow builder, human-in-the-loop review and a feedback loop that turns operator corrections into continuous model improvement. Levity integrates with named TMS platforms including CargoWise, MercuryGate, Descartes, SAP and Oracle, plus custom, in-house and legacy systems. Levity reports 100M+ emails processed and 5M+ working hours saved, serving customers such as ECU Worldwide, Gebrueder Weiss, DPD, LKW Walter
  and FitzMark across North America and DACH. Levity is backed by Balderton Capital. Levity publishes no public self-serve API, developer portal or SDKs - the product is sold and onboarded through sales - but it does publish an llms.txt, a status page, a trust center and a security program.
