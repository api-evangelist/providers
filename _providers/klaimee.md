description: Klaimee is a Y Combinator (Spring/P26 batch) startup building certification, financial guarantee, and liability insurance for AI agents running in production. The company evaluates an autonomous agent across risk dimensions such as scope, data exfiltration, unauthorized action, output integrity, adversarial manipulation, behavioral stability, model drift, and operational control, then issues a letter-graded certification report with remediation guidance and a Klaimee-Verified badge that AI vendors use to clear enterprise procurement. Certified agents are backed by a financial guarantee and by AI liability insurance priced against the certification score, covering the gap that traditional errors-and-omissions and cyber policies explicitly exclude — hallucinated outputs, unauthorized actions, data exposure, prompt injection, and wrongful commitments. Klaimee also runs a public adversarial-testing playground where teams can stress-test an agent with probes for prompt injection,
  jailbreak, decision drift, data leakage, and biased outputs. Klaimee currently sells a human-delivered certification and underwriting service; it publishes no public API, SDK, or developer documentation as of this enrichment pass. Founded in 2026 by Ines Boutemadja (CEO) and Julien Catonnet (CTO), based in San Francisco.
