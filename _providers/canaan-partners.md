description: Canaan Partners is an early-stage venture capital firm founded in 1987 as a management buyout spin-out from General Electric's venture unit. The firm invests in "visionaries with transformative ideas," backing companies from seed through Series A across a roughly 60/40 split between technology and healthcare. Headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut with offices in San Francisco, Menlo Park, and New York City, Canaan has raised roughly $7 billion across 13 funds and is actively deploying Fund XIII. Over its 37-year history the firm reports a track record of 73 IPOs and 152 M&A exits, with notable investments including Instacart, LendingClub, The RealReal, DexCom, Match.com, Snyk, Turo, Cardlytics, and Day One Biopharmaceuticals.
