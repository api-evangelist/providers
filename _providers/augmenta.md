description: Augmenta is a Toronto-based generative AI company building the Augmenta Construction Platform (ACP), a Spatial AI system that automates building design — starting with electrical raceway routing and expanding into mechanical and plumbing (MEP). ACP ingests Revit models, accepts conduit run schedules, fly/no-fly zones, and project specifications, then generates clash-free, constructable 3D conduit routes in the cloud and exports them back into Revit as system conduit ready for detailing and prefabrication. The platform combines computational geometry, advanced optimization, and machine learning to handle data-center, hospital, and school-scale electrical builds, and is marketed to VDC teams and electrical contractors as a way to cut BIM time by roughly two-thirds. Augmenta was founded by veterans of generative design for advanced manufacturing, won EC&M 2026 Product of the Year, was named to the 2026 Global Cleantech 100, and signed a five-year strategic partnership with E-J
