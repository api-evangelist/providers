description: Hatch Loyalty (formerly Belly) was a Chicago-based customer activation, loyalty, and engagement platform for enterprise retailers, with a strong focus on the fuel and convenience-store vertical. It aggregated customer data across a retailer's business systems to build customer profiles and ran a rules engine over submitted transactions to trigger offers and redemptions. Hatch exposed a RESTful developer platform (the "Hatch HUB" API) that conformed to the JSON:API specification, with a public developer portal, API reference, and transaction-processing tutorials. The company was backed by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Lightbank, NEA, and DAG Ventures, and was acquired by Stuzo around 2019-2020 (the earlier Belly product line was sold to Mobivity). As of this profile the hatchloyalty.com domain and all of its subdomains (www, developer, api) are parked and listed for sale, so no live API or developer surface remains to enrich.
