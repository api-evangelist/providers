description: Perimeter (formerly ProcessBolt) is a vendor risk management (VRM) platform built for regulated industries. It covers the full third-party risk lifecycle through six modules — Monitor (continuous vendor surveillance and alerts), Assess (automated risk assessments with a drag-and-drop template builder), Extract (AI-powered document intelligence), Verify (attack-surface validation), Share (a document management and collaboration hub), and Respond (RFP and assessment response automation). The product centralizes vendor information, scores and reports vendor risk, and supports no-code integration and API-based / CSV vendor imports across US, EU, and Australia data regions. ProcessBolt rebranded to Perimeter and now serves the platform from perimeter.net; processbolt.com redirects there. No public developer portal, OpenAPI, or SDKs are published — the platform's integration APIs are exposed to customers rather than through an open developer surface.
