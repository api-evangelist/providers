description: CYGNVS (an acronym for "CYber GuidaNce Virtual Space") is a Silicon Valley SaaS company that operates an out-of-band command center for cyber crisis preparedness and incident response. The platform runs independently of an organization's corporate infrastructure so that response teams — security, legal, executives, insurers, and external stakeholders — can coordinate securely even when internal systems are compromised or down, preserving legal privilege and chain of custody. CYGNVS provides guided playbooks, tabletop exercises, regulated-response execution with regulatory reporting templates across many jurisdictions, and bundles CISO personal-liability insurance underwritten by AIG.
