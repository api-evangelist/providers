It builds software infrastructure for quantum applications, centered on Triple Alpha, an integrated development environment that lets developers write hardware-agnostic quantum programs at multiple levels of abstraction and deploy them to quantum processors. Triple Alpha's final workflow step packages a program and deploys it as an API so developers can invoke quantum backends from Python scripts, web pages, and other technologies. Horizon operates its own quantum computing testbed at its Singapore headquarters and supports a range of external quantum processors, aiming to bridge the gap between today's quantum hardware and real-world applications.
