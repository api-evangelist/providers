description: SewerAI is a Walnut Creek, California software company (founded 2019, SewerAI Corp) building AI computer-vision and cloud asset-management software for underground wastewater infrastructure. Its platform pairs PIONEER, a cloud CCTV inspection and sewer asset-management environment, with AutoCode, a computer-vision engine that automatically assigns NASSCO observation codes to sewer pipe, lateral and manhole inspection video, plus Risk & Rehab, Sewer3D, QAI and Smart Project Builder. It is NASSCO-certified software for PACP, LACP and MACP import and export, and sells to municipal utilities, engineering firms and inspection contractors. SewerAI publishes a versioned REST Enterprise API (v1) at api.sewerai.com for creating organizations, assets, projects, inspections, observations and videos, uploading inspection footage via presigned S3 posts, kicking off AutoCode runs, and pulling coded results and exports back into GIS, CMMS and work-order systems such as Esri ArcGIS, Trimble
  Cityworks, OpenGov Cartegraph, Lucity, Infor and Unearth.
