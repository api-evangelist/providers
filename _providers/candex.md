description: Candex is a fintech "master vendor" platform that lets large enterprises engage, track, and pay long-tail, one-time, and hard-to-onboard suppliers across more than 50 countries without setting each vendor up in their own ERP or finance systems. A buyer configures Candex once; when it needs to purchase from a small or irregular supplier, it transacts with Candex, which handles supplier onboarding, tax and banking validation, compliance scans, PO and non-PO invoicing, and cross-border payment execution, then remits to the actual supplier. By consolidating tail-spend vendors and their records, Candex reduces administrative overhead, increases spend visibility and control, and makes "No PO, No Pay" policies achievable.
