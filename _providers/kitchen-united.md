Backed by GV (Google Ventures) among others, the company wound down its physical footprint: in late 2023 it announced it would sell or close all remaining units and pivot to licensing its ordering software, and on 2024-03-14 sbe/C3 founder Sam Nazarian acquired its intellectual property and ghost-kitchen assets into a new holding company, Everybody Eats, alongside C3 and Nextbite.
