description: Blue River Technology is an agricultural robotics and machine-learning company headquartered in Santa Clara, California, and a wholly owned subsidiary of John Deere (Deere & Company), which acquired it in 2017. Founded in 2011 by Jorge Heraud and Lee Redden out of Stanford University, the company builds intelligent machinery that combines computer vision, machine learning, robotics, and full-stack software to make farming more precise and sustainable. Its flagship product, See & Spray, uses onboard cameras and deep-learning models to distinguish crops from weeds in real time and target herbicide only where it is needed, dramatically reducing chemical use. Blue River operates as John Deere's autonomy and computer-vision engineering hub, applying the same perception and edge-AI stack across agriculture, construction, and mining equipment. The company does not publish a public developer API, SDK, or documentation portal; its technology ships embedded in John Deere machinery and
