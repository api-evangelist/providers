description: Toyota Connected is Toyota Motor Corporation's global connected mobility technology arm, building the data, telematics, and software platforms that power Toyota and Lexus connected vehicle services worldwide. The group operates as a federation of regional subsidiaries — TOYOTA Connected Corporation (Japan), Toyota Connected North America (TCNA, Plano, Texas), TOYOTA Connected Europe (TCEU, United Kingdom), TOYOTA Connected India (TCIN), TOYOTA Connected Asia Pacific, and Toyota Tsusho Connected Middle East — and runs Toyota's Mobility Service Platform (MSPF) and the Drivelink telematics backbone behind Safety Connect, Service Connect, Remote Connect, Destination Assist, Wi-Fi Connect, and the "Hey Toyota" in-vehicle voice assistant. TCNA reports surpassing five million connected customer calls across Toyota and Lexus and runs Toyota's Big Data Center for connected-vehicle data. KINTO — Toyota's mobility services brand for car subscription, car-sharing, ride-hailing, and carpooling
  (KINTO One, KINTO Flex, KINTO Share, KINTO Ride, KINTO Join) — sits adjacent in Toyota's mobility portfolio and is powered by KINTO Technologies on AWS. Toyota exposes developer portals at developer.eig.toyota.com (Toyota API Portal covering TMC, TMNA, TEMA, TFS, NAMC, and other entities) and developer.toyotaconnected.eu (Toyota Connected EU API services, including Telematics and Vehicle APIs documented with Swagger/OpenAPI), but access is gated to partners and internal Toyota teams — no public, unauthenticated OpenAPI specifications are published. Third-party access to Toyota vehicle data for individual developers is typically brokered through partners such as Smartcar and High Mobility rather than directly via Toyota's own portals.
