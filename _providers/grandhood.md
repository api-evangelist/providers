description: Grandhood is a Danish digital pension provider that lets companies set up and run a modern workplace (company) pension scheme entirely online, without the traditional adviser meetings and paperwork. Employers register with MitID, invite employees, and activate a plan; employees enroll, get built-in digital pension counselling, choose an investment profile, consolidate scattered pension savings, and see real-time savings, returns, and insurance coverage in the Grandhood mobile app. Pensions bundle both long-term savings and present-day insurance protection. Pricing is a simple fixed monthly amount per employee (65-95 DKK/month) plus investment costs, with no deposit-percentage fees and free pension advice. The underlying pension administration is backed by Velliv, Denmark's third-largest pension company. Grandhood is based in Copenhagen (Esplanaden 14B, 1263 København K; CVR 44147750) and is a portfolio company of Speedinvest. Grandhood publishes consumer web and mobile-app products;
