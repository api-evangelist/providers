description: The Human Utility (formerly the Detroit Water Project) is a Y Combinator-backed not-for-profit organization founded in 2014 by Tiffani Ashley Bell whose mission is to preserve human dignity by increasing water affordability for families across the United States. It matches families who are behind on their water bills with donors who want to help, paying utility companies directly on their behalf through crowdfunded donations, community-building, and policy action. Operating in Detroit, Baltimore, and dozens of other cities, it has helped thousands of households avoid water shutoffs. The Human Utility runs a public-facing web application for donors and recipients rather than a developer-facing API product; its source code lives in the humanutility GitHub organization.
