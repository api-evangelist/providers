description: AtheroMed, Inc. was a privately-held medical device company based in Menlo Park, California, founded to treat peripheral artery disease (PAD). Its sole product was the Phoenix Atherectomy System, an over-the-wire, low-profile atherectomy catheter that uses a rotating front-cutting element at the distal tip to shave diseased material directly into the catheter, where an internal Archimedes screw continuously captures and removes the debulked material without requiring specialized capital equipment. The Phoenix system received FDA 510(k) clearance in January 2014 and CE Mark approval in Europe. AtheroMed was venture-backed by Canaan Partners and U.S. Venture Partners, and was acquired by Volcano Corporation in June 2014 for $115 million upfront plus milestone payments; Volcano was in turn acquired by Royal Philips in February 2015. AtheroMed no longer operates as an independent company, its atheromed.com domain is parked and listed for sale, and it has no developer program, public
