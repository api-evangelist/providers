description: Duco is an AI-powered operational data automation platform for financial services. It helps banks, asset managers, brokers, asset servicers and market infrastructure firms normalize, validate and reconcile any data in the cloud — cutting the time spent on manual data work, reducing operational risk and accelerating front-, middle- and back-office processes. The no-code platform ingests data from any source (including unstructured documents via Adaptive Intelligent Document Processing), then transforms, matches, reconciles and publishes it with full audit and control.
