description: Mukuru is a Cape Town-headquartered, pan-African fintech and one of the continent's largest formal remittance providers, serving over 16 million customers across Southern, Eastern and Western Africa, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. Founded in 2004 to make sending money home faster and more affordable for the African diaspora, Mukuru now operates a broader financial services platform spanning international and domestic money transfers, the Mukuru Card (a prepaid card product), Mukuru Wallet (a mobile money wallet available in markets such as Botswana for airtime, electricity, and bill payments), Mukuru Pay (cash-based online checkout at retail partners), and Mukuru Funeral Cover (life insurance with cross-border repatriation benefits underwritten by Guardrisk Life). The company is licensed as a South African Financial Services Provider (FSP 45517) and registered Credit Provider (NCRCP9244), and reaches customers through a hybrid network of branches, retail booths,
  USSD, WhatsApp, web, and native Android, iOS, and Huawei AppGallery apps, supported by more than 320,000 pay-in and pay-out locations. An enterprise arm — branded under the Mukuru Enterprise / MukuruPay umbrella — offers collections and disbursements rails to businesses sending funds into or paying out across Africa. Mukuru does not publish a public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDK, or open-source client; its Mukuru GitHub organization contains only archived forks of upstream infrastructure tooling (Drone, Helm, Puppet, PHP Docker images) and no Mukuru-authored libraries. Integrations with the Mukuru Enterprise platform are arranged through direct commercial engagement rather than a self-service developer program.
