description: Airware was an enterprise commercial-drone (UAV) software and aerial-data-analytics company founded in 2011 by Jonathan Downey and headquartered in San Francisco. It began by building an operating system and autopilot for commercial drones, then pivoted to a cloud-based "Aerial Information Platform" that captured, processed, and analyzed drone imagery for enterprise verticals such as mining, quarrying, insurance, and construction. Airware acquired French drone-analytics firm Redbird in 2016 and raised roughly $118M from investors including Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Kleiner Perkins, GV (Google Ventures), Intel Capital, and Next World Capital. The company ceased operations in September 2018.
