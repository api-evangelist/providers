description: Alexi is a Toronto, Ontario, Canada based legal artificial intelligence company that operates a private AI platform purpose-built for law firms, unifying legal research, drafting, and analysis into a single, single-tenant environment. Founded in 2017 by Mark Doble and a team of lawyers and technologists, Alexi specializes in litigation workflows — generating research memos grounded in primary law, identifying controlling authority and jurisdiction-specific requirements, framing case strategy, and producing persuasive first drafts that reflect a firm's voice, citation style, and approved structures. The platform also supports transactional work, document review and comparison, knowledge management, and firm-specific playbook application. Alexi's architecture combines a retrieval-first approach grounded in cited authority with agentic reasoning, delivered through a private cloud deployment so that firms retain ownership and sovereignty over their data, models, and workflows. Coverage
  spans all 50 U.S. states, Washington D.C., federal law, and all Canadian provinces and territories (excluding Quebec). The platform integrates with 20+ tools including iManage, NetDocuments, SharePoint, Elite 3E, Aderant, Litify, Actionstep, Filevine, Microsoft Word, Excel, Teams, Outlook, Zoom, DocuSign, Adobe Sign, Azure, AWS, Datasite, Intralinks, and Firmex. Alexi is SOC 2 certified, employs AES-256 encryption, and reports 99% uptime with customer firms including Dentons, Robins Appleby, Gluckstein Lawyers, MacLean Law, and Mongillo Law. The company's revenue model is SaaS subscription (Alexi Core and Alexi Advanced tiers); there is no public developer API, SDK, or open-source release, and integrations are delivered as turnkey enterprise connectors rather than a publicly documented integration platform.
