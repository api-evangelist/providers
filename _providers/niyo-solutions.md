Niyo is not a chartered bank; it operates as a neo-banking front end layered on partner RBI-licensed banks — DCB Bank (current flagship for the Niyo zero-forex savings + debit card), SBM Bank India (previous "Niyo Global" forex card, now wound down for new customers and operating a secured credit card variant), Equitas Small Finance Bank (salary / "Niyo Bharat" account) and IDFC FIRST Bank (earlier Niyo Global issuer) — plus card network rails (Visa, RuPay). Niyo''s flagship surface is the "zero forex markup" Niyo Global / DCB Niyo Global / SBM Niyo Global Visa debit and prepaid travel cards accepted in 180+ countries, paired with a digital savings account delivered through partner banks. The company has expanded beyond cards into a full travel-banking stack including
  currency exchange and forex cash delivery, outbound remittance (Send Money Abroad), international flights, hotels and curated experiences, Niyo Express airport services, an international travel eSIM, travel visa assistance, travel insurance, a premium subscription tier, a student program (Niyo Scholarship, NISAP) and a DCB Niyo Secured Credit Card backed by a fixed deposit. Niyo serves over one million international travellers and has raised capital from investors including Accel, Lightrock, Beams Fintech Fund, Tencent, Horizons Ventures, Prime Venture Partners and JS Capital. There is no public REST developer API, partner banking-as-a-service surface, SDK, MCP server, or GitHub organisation — Niyo is delivered exclusively as a B2C iOS and Android mobile application with a marketing and tools website at goniyo.com.'
