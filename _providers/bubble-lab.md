description: Bubble Lab is an open-core, agentic workflow automation platform built for developers. Its open-source (Apache-2.0) TypeScript engine lets you compose AI agents and integrations as typed, composable units called "Bubbles" — service bubbles (Slack, Gmail, PostgreSQL, Google Sheets, Airtable, ElevenLabs, Resend, and more) and tool bubbles (web scraping, research agents, SQL, LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube) — then stitches them into production-grade "BubbleFlow" pipelines with end-to-end type safety, built-in logging, tracing, and step-level observability. Workflows are fully runnable, self-hostable, and exportable to your own backend, or authored visually in the hosted Bubble Studio. Scaffold a project in seconds with the create-bubblelab-app CLI.
