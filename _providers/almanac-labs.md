description: Almanac Labs, Inc. builds Almanac, an enterprise wiki and async collaboration platform that consolidates a team's scattered documents into a single, organized source of truth. Almanac combines fast document editing, wikis, version control, approvals, and workflow/task management to replace tools like Confluence, Notion, and Asana, and it publishes company handbooks and community-powered documentation. The Almanac API (V1, private beta) lets workspaces import and move files and folders, view users and groups, read and update document properties, manage who has access to documents, and access and update metadata for documents and folders.
