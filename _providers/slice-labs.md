description: Slice Labs is a New York-based insurtech company that operates as an insurance-as-a-service cloud platform provider. Founded in 2015 and publicly launched in 2016, Slice originally offered on-demand insurance products for the sharing economy (homeshare, rideshare, cyber) and then pivoted to offering its underlying digital insurer stack — branded Insurance Cloud Services (ICS) — to other carriers, MGAs, and brands.
