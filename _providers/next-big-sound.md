description: Next Big Sound was a music-industry analytics company founded in 2009 out of Techstars Boulder that tracked artists' social, streaming, and airplay signals (plays, fans, engagement across Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, radio and social platforms) and exposed them through a REST developer API used by labels, managers and media. Its analytics became Pandora's artist tools and were folded into the Pandora / SiriusXM Artist Marketing Platform (AMP). The standalone developer API and portal (developer.nextbigsound.com) have been deprecated; nextbigsound.com now resolves to Pandora infrastructure and HTTP redirects to amp.pandora.com.
