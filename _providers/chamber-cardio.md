description: Chamber Cardio is a Washington, D.C.-based healthcare technology company building a cardiology-focused platform for value-based care. Chamber partners with cardiology practices and health plans to align clinical signals, workflows, and patient data so cardiologists can identify high-risk patients earlier, close gaps against treatment guidelines, and reduce administrative burden. Its workflow-integrated AI surfaces clearer signal from the large volumes of cardiovascular data generated in care, supporting better patient outcomes and financial predictability for payers as cardiology moves beyond fee-for-service.
