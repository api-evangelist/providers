description: Tactic was a cryptocurrency and digital-asset accounting platform founded in 2021 in New York and backed by Lux Capital. Its product aggregated on-chain activity and exchange/custody data (Coinbase and others) into a unified treasury view and crypto sub-ledger, automatically categorizing transactions, calculating USD gain/loss and taxable events, and reconciling the crypto sub-ledger to traditional accounting software such as QuickBooks. Tactic launched from stealth in 2022 (originally on choosetactic.com, later the exact-match tactic.com) and raised roughly $13.6M before being acquired by TaxBit in January 2023 to augment TaxBit's digital-asset accounting suite. The standalone product, developer portal, and API surface have been retired; the tactic.com domain is now listed for sale and no independent developer surface remains.
