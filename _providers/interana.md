description: Interana was an event-based behavioral analytics platform for interactively querying large-scale event data (clickstream, product usage, and time-series data) at full resolution. Founded around 2013 in Redwood City, California by former Facebook engineers, it was used at scale by companies including Microsoft Bing. The company's team and technology were acquired by Twitter in March 2020; the standalone product was spun out and continued as Scuba Analytics (scuba.io), and the interana.com domain now redirects to its successor lineage. This API Evangelist profile is retained as a historical / defunct company record — there is no live public API surface, developer portal, or hosted documentation remaining.
