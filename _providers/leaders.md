description: Leade.rs, Inc. was a two-sided marketplace that matched keynote, motivational, and business speakers with the conferences, corporate events, brands, and press that wanted to book them. Event organizers could search a curated roster and create an event, while speakers maintained a profile covering their availability and topics — the platform's stated aim was to remove the cold-outreach grind from conference programming and surface newer and more diverse voices. The company was co-founded by Loïc Le Meur, the French entrepreneur behind the LeWeb conference and a Silicon Valley angel investor; at acquisition its network was reported at roughly 300 speakers across 11 core fields including crypto, AI, and entrepreneurship. ASX-listed Animoca Brands acquired 100% of the equity on 6 May 2019 for US$0.5 million upfront, intending to grow the roster into a mentor and investor network bridging East and West alongside its Zeroth accelerator, with Le Meur joining Animoca as a venture partner.
  The product did not survive the acquisition. Leade.rs never published a public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable documentation, and as of the July 2026 enrichment pass the leade.rs domain no longer resolves — it remains registered through December 2026 and is delegated to EuroDNS nameservers, but the zone is not served, so there is no live surface of any kind to harvest.
